Week 1 Leadership Under Christ

Week 1 — Leadership Under Christ

Week 1 establishes that Knightly leadership begins as stewardship under Christ, not status, pride, or ceremony. The first movement of Knight formation is inward: monk before warrior, consecration before recognition, surrender before public responsibility.

Primary Focus

Monk before warrior; inward consecration before outward rank; surrender of personal ambition; monastic foundation of the original Templar Order; hidden devotion and private prayer; dying to self in order to live for Christ.

Reflection Questions
  • Where are you seeking outward recognition over inward purity?
  • What does "monk before warrior" demand of you personally?
Discussion Questions
  • Why is a warrior without the discipline of a monk dangerous to the Order and the Church?
  • How do we maintain inward consecration in a culture obsessed with outward success?
Readiness Goal

Demonstrate a sincere surrender of personal ambition and an embrace of hidden, inward devotion.

Week 2 Leadership Inside KTKC

Week 2 — Leadership Inside KTKC

Week 2 focuses on leadership inside the Order through brotherhood, accountability, consistency, service, and honest conduct. The Knight must be governed inwardly before he can be trusted outwardly.

Primary Focus

Prayer, Scripture, obedience, discipline, accountability, self-mastery; establishing a daily rhythm of prayer and Scripture; obedience to legitimate authority; self-mastery over appetites, time, and desires; transparent accountability.

Reflection Questions
  • Where does your current daily routine lack spiritual discipline?
  • How do you respond internally to an order or directive you dislike?
Discussion Questions
  • How does a structured Rule of Life create spiritual freedom rather than legalism?
  • What role does accountability play in preventing moral failure in leadership?
Readiness Goal

Establish and articulate a rigorous personal Rule of Life anchored in daily spiritual discipline.

Week 3 Christian Witness in Modern Society

Week 3 — Christian Witness in Modern Society

Week 3 trains the Knight to apply Christian leadership in family life, church life, work, speech, and public witness. This week pushes toward spiritual warfare, steadfastness, humility, and final readiness for knightly responsibility.

Primary Focus

Spiritual warfare, steadfastness, service, leadership through humility, final readiness for knightly responsibility; putting on the full armor of God; servant leadership; steadfastness in trial; final self-examination.

Reflection Questions
  • Are you prepared to accept the burdens of leadership without the praises of men?
  • What vulnerabilities currently exist in your spiritual armor?
Discussion Questions
  • What does it practically mean to engage in spiritual warfare today?
  • How will you ensure that promotion to Knight results in greater service rather than greater pride?
Readiness Goal

Complete final self-examination confirming absolute readiness for solemn knightly vows and selfless service.

Week 4 Day of Service and Leadership in Action

Week 4 — Day of Service and Leadership in Action

Week 4 puts leadership into action through planning or completing a Day of Service project rooted in Christian charity and public witness. Leadership is tested not only in conviction, but in faithful, visible service carried out with humility and consistency.

Primary Focus

Leadership through service, practical charity, reliability in visible works, and faithful public witness. This week moves the Knight from internal preparation toward concrete, accountable action.

Reflection Questions
  • How does your willingness to serve reveal the true condition of your heart?
  • Where do convenience, pride, or hesitation weaken your readiness to serve others?
Discussion Questions
  • Why must Christian leadership be demonstrated in service rather than claimed in title alone?
  • How does a Day of Service expose whether a man is dependable in action and not only in speech?
Readiness Goal

Plan or complete a Day of Service project with clear charity, order, follow-through, and accountability.
