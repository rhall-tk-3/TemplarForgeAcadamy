Knight Major Handbook

Purpose of the Academy and Knight Major Handbook

This handbook establishes the operational guidelines, curriculum standards, and leadership expectations for the Knight Major rank within the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ - Templar Forge Academy. The Academy is strictly educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose. The Knight Major program serves as an advanced formation phase for senior officers entrusted with safeguarding the overarching governance and instructional integrity of the institution.

It must be explicitly stated that the Order and its Academy are not military or paramilitary in nature; internal ranks are organizational and instructional only. The program exists to forge individuals of strong moral character dedicated to charitable works, orderly administration, and rigorous spiritual formation, completely devoid of martial posturing or unofficial military style.

Mission and Historical Foundation

The primary mission of the Templar Forge Academy is to cultivate a truthful understanding of the medieval Knights Templar, rejecting conjecture and myth in favor of genuine historical appreciation and spiritual emulation. The Order is forever consecrated to Jesus Christ, and this dedication shapes our modern mission to provide a truthful, nonviolent witness to His gospel through sacrificial service, mentorship, and brotherhood.

The historical foundation of the Knight Major formation draws upon the immense responsibilities of the senior officers and regional Masters in the original Templar Order. It examines how true leadership at the highest levels requires the steadfast preservation of doctrine and standards, strengthening subordinates, and ensuring that the organization remains anchored to its founding holy purpose amidst external pressures and internal growth.

Core Principles

The Knight Major program requires strict adherence to the core principles that govern the conduct, administration, and daily lives of our senior leaders. Candidates are trained to embody:

  • Truth before legend: Committing to rigorous, source-based historical analysis and preserving factual history over popular, romanticized fabrications.
  • Service before status: Embracing the principle of service before self, viewing senior leadership as an expansion of one's capacity to serve and protect the organization rather than a mechanism for privilege.
  • Discipline before display: Emphasizing internal spiritual mastery, orderly administration, and obedience over outward organizational pageantry.
  • Charity before privilege: Ensuring that the resources, influence, and efforts of the Academy are efficiently directed toward those in genuine need.
  • Leadership through accountability: Recognizing that no member is above reproach, and all are accountable to God, Scripture, and the fellowship of the Order. Leaders are held to a higher standard of visible virtue and humility.

Program Audience and Scope

The Knight Major handbook functions as a senior adult leadership guide specifically designed for members who have been entrusted with academy-wide standards oversight. The scope of this role encompasses curriculum governance, officer evaluation, long-range strategic planning, and the absolute protection of institutional integrity.

Program Standards and Legitimacy Measures

Institutional integrity is guarded through strict adherence to established program standards. The Academy utilizes signed enrollment records, direct Schoolmaster oversight, written course standards, formal rank boards, and an annual curriculum review to maintain the highest quality of formation. Reading requirements and mandatory service work are integrated directly into the leadership curriculum.

The Templar Forge Academy enforces a clear and absolute rejection of false lineage claims, invented authority, theatrical succession claims, and any unofficial military style. We are educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose. Any attempt to militarize the Order or use ranks for anything other than organizational and instructional purposes is strictly prohibited.

Leadership Expectations and Records Maintained

Knight Majors are the senior administrators of formation, governance, and institutional health. They are expected to maintain the highest standards of rigorous oversight, participation, and selfless mentorship. To ensure these standards are met, the Academy requires the meticulous maintenance of the following records:

  • Attendance logs
  • Reading logs
  • Service logs
  • Assessment sheets
  • Oral-board notes
  • Advancement recommendations
  • Chapter reports
  • Curriculum review notes
  • Officer evaluations
  • Annual planning records
  • Mentorship records

Assessment Model

Candidates for Knight Major are continually evaluated based on five foundational pillars of assessment:

  • Knowledge: Absolute mastery of overarching curriculum standards, historical accuracy, and the doctrinal consistency of the Order.
  • Judgment: The exercise of deeply tested Christian discernment in resolving broad organizational conflicts, guiding boards, and ensuring institutional fairness.
  • Service: Proven capability in aligning academy-wide charitable efforts, coordinating service campaigns, and supporting chapter operations.
  • Character: Unwavering demonstration of visible humility, calm corrective leadership under institutional strain, and profound moral integrity.
  • Communication: The ability to articulate truthful public communication, effectively evaluate subordinate officers, and preserve the Academy's reputation.

Full 8-Week Knight Major Curriculum

Week 1: Office of the Knight Major

Primary Focus

Scope of senior authority, governance responsibilities, boundaries of oversight, and stewardship without domination.

Key Leadership Subjects

Understanding the specific mandate of the Major's office; distinguishing governance from micromanagement; the sacred trust of senior leadership.

Formation Emphasis

Internalizing the concept of servant leadership at the highest levels; bearing authority with humility.

Readiness Goal

Clearly articulate the boundaries, duties, and heavy stewardship required of the Knight Major office.

Week 2: Curriculum Governance and Standards Protection

Primary Focus

Written standards, curriculum review, doctrinal consistency, historical accuracy, and instructional integrity.

Key Leadership Subjects

Auditing and updating Academy texts; ensuring historical teachings reject myth; enforcing instructional uniformity across chapters.

Formation Emphasis

Submission to truth over convenience; protecting the intellectual and spiritual core of the Academy.

Readiness Goal

Successfully audit a portion of the curriculum and present a comprehensive standards review.

Week 3: Officer Evaluation and Development

Primary Focus

Evaluating Captains and Schoolmasters, strengthening weak areas, succession planning, and mentoring leaders.

Key Leadership Subjects

Conducting fair performance evaluations; building reliable leadership pipelines; advising and correcting subordinate officers.

Formation Emphasis

Rejoicing in the success of others; building a robust organization rather than a personal fiefdom.

Readiness Goal

Develop a strategic mentorship and succession plan for a hypothetical chapter facing leadership transition.

Week 4: Boards, Reviews, and Institutional Accountability

Primary Focus

Rank boards, review processes, documented accountability, appeals, fairness, and integrity in decisions.

Key Leadership Subjects

Chairing promotion and disciplinary boards; handling member appeals; maintaining impeccable administrative records.

Formation Emphasis

Practicing justice and mercy in equal measure; avoiding favoritism and bias.

Readiness Goal

Demonstrate proficiency in leading a rank board review and documenting outcomes with clarity and fairness.

Week 5: Strategic Planning and Annual Academy Coordination

Primary Focus

Long-range planning, annual cycle management, service alignment, chapter support, and administrative order.

Key Leadership Subjects

Mapping the yearly academic and service calendar; aligning chapter resources for maximum charitable impact; providing logistical support frameworks.

Formation Emphasis

Translating spiritual and educational vision into sustained, long-term operational reality.

Readiness Goal

Draft a comprehensive annual strategic plan for the Academy, balancing instruction, service, and officer retreats.

Week 6: Public Credibility, Historical Integrity, and External Representation

Primary Focus

Truthful public communication, legitimacy measures, rejecting false lineage claims, and protecting the Academy's reputation.

Key Leadership Subjects

Guarding the public face of the Order; addressing external inquiries; firmly countering theatrical claims of Templar succession.

Formation Emphasis

Embracing the humility of historical truth; standing firm against the allure of esoteric or invented grandeur.

Readiness Goal

Draft an official public statement that effectively communicates the true history and modern purpose of the Order.

Week 7: Crisis Oversight, Correction, and Burden Bearing

Primary Focus

Conflict across chapters, officer failure, doctrinal drift, institutional strain, and calm corrective leadership.

Key Leadership Subjects

Managing severe organizational fatigue; intervening in chapter-level disputes; restoring order after leadership failures.

Formation Emphasis

Leaning heavily on Christ as the ultimate foundation during periods of deep institutional stress.

Readiness Goal

Exhibit calm, decisive, and prayerful strategies for resolving complex, multi-chapter crisis scenarios.

Week 8: Annual Review, Major Readiness, and Faithful Continuity

Primary Focus

Final review, recommendations, succession, long-term faithfulness, and continuity of standards.

Key Leadership Subjects

Ensuring the seamless handoff of duties; finalizing annual reports; maintaining the unbroken continuity of Academy standards.

Formation Emphasis

Cultivating a mindset of quiet obedience and generational faithfulness to the mission of the Order.

Readiness Goal

Complete the Knight Major formation cycle, presenting all required governance documentation and demonstrating readiness for senior office.

Schoolmaster Guidance

The office of Knight Major is a profound stewardship office of trust, not a ceremonial distinction or a reward for mere longevity. Senior reviewers evaluating candidates for this rank must rigorously examine the individual's record over a sustained period. Look deeply for visible humility, long-tested judgment, orderly administration, historical integrity, and absolute fairness in evaluation.

The true mark of a Knight Major is the ability to preserve overarching standards without seeking self-exaltation. It must be stated plainly: a man who desires control more than service is fundamentally not ready for this office.

Promotion Readiness Statement

Completion of the 8-week Knight Major formation cycle is strictly required, but it does not guarantee automatic recognition or continuation in senior office. The elevation to or confirmation of this rank involves rigorous evaluation by the highest governing boards. This evaluation comprehensively considers coursework completion, logged attendance, active participation, curriculum stewardship, officer evaluation ability, disciplined conduct, institutional faithfulness, and overall conduct becoming of a senior member.

It is an inviolable rule of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ that ranks cannot be bought or sold. Senior authority is earned exclusively through demonstrated virtue, unwavering competence, and tested faithfulness.

Suggested Annual Academy Cycle

To sustain the overarching health of the Academy and the continuing education of its leaders, the Knight Major operates within a strategic annual academy cycle:

  • One annual standards review term dedicated to auditing and improving instruction.
  • One curriculum revision and planning term to ensure historical and doctrinal consistency.
  • One officer development retreat for the spiritual renewal and training of Captains and Schoolmasters.
  • One academy-wide service campaign alignment review.
  • One annual board and reporting cycle to process evaluations and institutional planning.
  • One recognition or recommissioning ceremony to honor faithful service and install new leadership.
