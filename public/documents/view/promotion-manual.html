Promotion Manual and By-Laws
Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ

ARTICLE I

PURPOSE AND AUTHORITY

Section 1.1 — Purpose

These By-Laws establish the official order of promotion within the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ (KTKC), as set forth by the leadership of the Order. These provisions define the standards, timelines, participation requirements, coursework obligations, attendance expectations, and review procedures governing advancement from initial membership through the rank of Knight.

Section 1.2 — Authority

All promotions within KTKC are subject to the authority of the Order’s leadership and the Board of Promotion. Promotion is not automatic, but is granted only upon satisfactory completion of all required obligations and favorable determination by the proper reviewing authority.

Section 1.3 — Applicability

These provisions apply to all admitted members of the Order who are progressing through the established ranks of Levies, Corporal, Sergeant, Sergeant First Class, Knight Aspirant, Knight, Knight Lieutenant, Knight Captain, Knight Major, and Knight Commander.

ARTICLE II

GENERAL PRINCIPLES OF PROMOTION

Section 2.1 — Basis of Advancement

Promotion within the Order shall be based upon the member’s demonstrated faithfulness, discipline, participation, and successful completion of all assigned requirements for the rank under review.

Section 2.2 — Minimum Standards

To be considered for promotion, each member shall be expected to demonstrate the following:

Section 2.2.1 Completion of required class instruction within the prescribed timeframe.

Section 2.2.2 Compliance with the Rules, Regulations, and Operating Procedures of the Order.

Section 2.2.3 Regular attendance at meetings, Bible studies, and General Order Meetings, also known as “Round Tables.”

Section 2.2.4 Active participation in the life, fellowship, and work of the Order.

Section 2.2.5 Conduct becoming of a member preparing for greater responsibility and honor within KTKC.

Section 2.3 — Promotion Not Automatic

Completion of time in rank alone shall not guarantee advancement. Each member shall remain subject to review of conduct, participation, attendance, coursework completion, and overall readiness.

ARTICLE III

ATTENDANCE, PARTICIPATION, AND RECORDKEEPING

Section 3.1 — Attendance Requirement

Attendance shall be required as part of active participation in the Order. Members shall be expected to regularly attend scheduled meetings, Bible studies, and General Order Meetings, also referred to as Round Tables.

Section 3.2 — Attendance Log

Attendance shall be logged and recorded by the Order to show active participation in meetings, Bible study, and general Order meetings, also known as Round Tables.

Section 3.3 — Use of Attendance Records

Attendance records shall be considered as part of the promotion review process and may be used by leadership and the Board of Promotion in determining whether a member has remained active and engaged.

Section 3.4 — Participation Standard

A member’s attendance record, participation in classwork, and visible fellowship within the Order shall together serve as evidence of diligence, commitment, and readiness for advancement.

Section 3.5 — Deficiency in Attendance

Irregular attendance, repeated absence, or lack of active participation may result in delayed promotion, extended review, or other corrective action as deemed appropriate by leadership and the Board of Promotion.

ARTICLE IV

RANK OF LEVIES

Section 4.1 — Probationary Period

A member entering the rank of Levies shall serve a probationary period of sixty (60) days, also recognized as an eight (8) week class period.

Section 4.2 — Required Instruction

During the probationary period, the member shall complete instruction in the following:

Section 4.2.1 Rules

Section 4.2.2 Regulations

Section 4.2.3 Operating Procedures

Section 4.3 — Standard for Promotion

Promotion from the rank of Levies shall require successful completion of the probationary period and completion of all required instruction within the allotted timeframe.

Section 4.4 — Consequence of Non-Completion

Failure to complete the required classwork within the sixty-day probationary period shall be reflected in the member’s Board of Promotion review and may delay advancement toward knighthood or any subsequent rank.

ARTICLE V

RANK OF CORPORAL

Section 5.1 — Class Timeline

A member seeking promotion to the rank of Corporal shall complete the required eight (8) week class portion.

Section 5.2 — Promotion Standards

Promotion to Corporal shall be based upon the following:

Section 5.2.1 Completion of the 8-week class requirement.

Section 5.2.2 Ability to remain current with all assigned classwork.

Section 5.2.3 Regular attendance at meetings, Bible studies, and Round Tables.

Section 5.2.4 Active fellowship and social interaction with other members of the Order.

Section 5.3 — Consequence of Deficiency

Failure to satisfy the above standards may be reflected in the member’s Board of Promotion review and may result in delayed promotion.

ARTICLE VI

RANK OF SERGEANT

Section 6.1 — Class Timeline

A member seeking promotion to the rank of Sergeant shall complete the required eight (8) week class portion.

Section 6.2 — Promotion Standards

Promotion to Sergeant shall be evaluated according to the same standards applicable to Corporal, including timely class completion, consistent attendance, and active participation in the Order.

Section 6.3 — Consequence of Deficiency

Failure to satisfy these requirements may be reflected in the member’s Board of Promotion review and may delay advancement.

ARTICLE VII

RANK OF SERGEANT FIRST CLASS

Section 7.1 — Class Timeline

A member seeking promotion to the rank of Sergeant First Class (SFC) shall complete the required eight (8) week class portion.

Section 7.2 — Promotion Standards

Promotion to Sergeant First Class shall be evaluated according to the same standards applicable to Sergeant, including:

Section 7.2.1 Completion of all required coursework

Section 7.2.2 Timely progress in assigned instruction

Section 7.2.3 Regular attendance at meetings, Bible studies, and Round Tables

Section 7.2.4 Active involvement in the fellowship and work of the Order

Section 7.3 — Consequence of Deficiency

Failure to satisfy these requirements may be reflected in the member’s Board of Promotion review and may delay advancement.

ARTICLE VIII

RANK OF KNIGHT ASPIRANT

Section 8.1 — Completion Period

A member holding the rank of Knight Aspirant shall be granted eight (8) weeks to complete all assigned classes and aspirant requirements.

Section 8.2 — Coursework Requirement

The Knight Aspirant shall complete all assigned classwork as directed by leadership.

Section 8.3 — Essay Requirement

The Knight Aspirant shall submit an essay entitled: “What Does a Templar Knight Mean to You?”

Section 8.4 — Essay Standards

Section 8.4.1 Standard college essay format

Section 8.4.2 Cover page required

Section 8.4.3 Minimum length of one full paper, front and back

Section 8.4.4 Single-spaced formatting

Section 8.5 — Review for Promotion to Knight

Promotion from Knight Aspirant to Knight shall be determined upon review of the following:

Section 8.5.1 Completion of all classwork within the prescribed timeline

Section 8.5.2 Completion of all Knight Aspirant requirements

Section 8.5.3 Submission of the required essay

Section 8.5.4 Attendance and active participation as reflected in official records

Section 8.5.5 Overall readiness as determined by the Board of Promotion

Section 8.6 — Notification of Determination

Once a final determination has been made, the Knight Aspirant shall be notified by telephone call by either the Temple Master of State, High Master of Temples, or the Grand Master.

ARTICLE IX

KNIGHTLY RANK STRUCTURE AND AUTHORITY

Section 9.1 – Purpose

This Article shall govern the description of knight ranks, and the standards for advancement within the Knight ranks of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ.

Section 9.2 – Knight Ranks

The Knightly ranks governed by this Article shall include the following:

Section 2.1 Knight

Section 2.2 Knight Lieutenant

Section 2.3 Knight Captain

Section 2.4 Knight Major

Section 2.5 Knight Commander

Section 9.3 – Promotion Advancement

Promotion within the Knight ranks shall not be automatic. Advancement shall be granted only upon fulfillment of all required standards and successful review by the Promotion Board, subject to final approval by KTKC leadership.

Section 9.4 – Promotion Review

All ranks within the Order, including all Knight ranks, shall be subject to review by the Promotion Board for both promotion and demotion.

Section 9.5 -- General Knightly Promotion Standards

Any member seeking advancement within the Knightly ranks shall be required to demonstrate faithfulness, discipline, maturity, service, active participation, and readiness for increased responsibility within the Order.

Section 9.5.1 All candidates for Knightly promotion shall complete all required classes within a period of one year or twelve months.

Section 9.5.2 All candidates for Knightly promotion shall complete the objectives set forth by the High Turcopolier and-or the Master of the Temples.

Section 9.5.3 All candidates for Knightly promotion shall attend all required meetings according to the same core attendance standards required of the non-commissioned ranks, including meetings, unless previously excused by proper authority.

Section 9.5.4 All candidates for Knightly promotion shall remain in good standing with all members of the Order and shall demonstrate conduct becoming of the rank sought.

Section 9.5.5 All Knightly promotions shall be subject to review by the Promotion Board. No member shall advance in Knightly rank without successfully passing Promotion Board review.

Section 9.5.6 Each Knightly rank shall require the minimum years of service within the Order.

Section 9.5.7 For promotion above the rank of Knight, a member shall have held the immediately preceding Knightly rank for a minimum of one year, unless otherwise directed by lawful authority of the Order.

Section 9.6 -- Specific Knightly Rank Requirements

Section 9.6.1 A member seeking promotion to the rank of Knight shall satisfy the following requirements:

Section 9.6.1.1 Complete all required classes within one year or twelve months.

Section 9.6.1.2 Complete all objectives established by the High Turcopolier and-or Master of the Temples.

Section 9.6.1.3 Have a minimum of one year of service within the Order.

Section 9.6.1.4 Attend all required meetings in accordance with non-commissioned core standards, unless previously excused.

Section 9.6.1.5 Remain in good standing with all members of the Order.

Section 9.6.1.6 Pass review before the Promotion Board.

Section 9.6.2 A member seeking promotion to the rank of Knight Lieutenant shall satisfy all requirements for Knightly promotion and shall additionally:

Section 9.6.2.1 Have held the rank of Knight for a minimum of one year.

Section 9.6.2.2 Have a minimum of two years of service within the Order.

Section 9.6.2.3 Pass review before the Promotion Board.

Section 9.6.3 A member seeking promotion to the rank of Knight Captain shall satisfy all requirements for Knightly promotion and shall additionally:

Section 9.6.3.1 Have held the rank of Knight Lieutenant for a minimum of one year.

Section 9.6.3.2 Have a minimum of three years of service within the Order.

Section 9.6.3.3 Pass review before the Promotion Board.

Section 9.6.4 A member seeking promotion to the rank of Knight Major shall satisfy all requirements for Knightly promotion and shall additionally:

Section 9.6.4.1 Have held the rank of Knight Captain for a minimum of one year.

Section 9.6.4.2 Have a minimum of four years of service within the Order.

Section 9.6.4.3 Pass review before the Promotion Board.

Section 9.6.5 A member seeking promotion to the rank of Knight Commander shall satisfy all requirements for Knightly promotion and shall additionally:

Section 9.6.4.1 Have held the rank of Knight Major for a minimum of one year.

Section 9.6.4.2 Have a minimum of five years of service within the Order.

Section 9.6.4.3 Pass review before the Promotion Board.

ARTICLE X

BOARD OF PROMOTION REVIEW

Section 10.1 — Function of the Board

The Board of Promotion shall review candidates for advancement based upon coursework completion, attendance, participation, conduct, and readiness for the rank sought.

Section 10.2 — Matters for Consideration

The Board may consider the following in all promotion reviews:

Section 10.2.1 Whether all coursework was completed within the required timeframe

Section 10.2.2 Whether attendance logs reflect active participation

Section 10.2.3 Whether the member has remained current in assignments

Section 10.2.4 Whether the member has demonstrated consistency, maturity, and fellowship

Section 10.2.5 Whether any deficiencies remain unresolved

Section 10.3 — Board Determinations

The Board of Promotion may approve advancement, delay promotion, extend the member’s review period, or require correction of deficiencies before advancement is reconsidered.

ARTICLE XI

DISCIPLINARY AND DEFICIENCY REVIEW STEPS

Section 11.1 — Deficiency Noted

A deficiency shall be noted when a member fails to complete assigned coursework, maintain regular attendance, satisfy rank-specific requirements, or uphold the standards of active participation required by the Order.

Section 11.2 — Preliminary Leadership Review

Upon discovery of a deficiency, the member’s status shall be reviewed by appropriate leadership to determine the nature and extent of the deficiency.

Section 11.3 — Referral to the Board of Promotion

If the deficiency is not corrected within a reasonable timeframe, the matter shall be referred to the Board of Promotion for formal review.

Section 11.4 — Corrective Action

Following review, the Board may impose one or more of the following actions:

Section 11.4.1 Delay of promotion

Section 11.4.2 Extension of probationary or completion period

Section 11.4.3 Requirement to complete outstanding classwork or assignments

Section 11.4.4 Additional period of observation and review

Section 11.5 — Reconsideration

A member who corrects all noted deficiencies may be reconsidered for promotion at a later date, subject to leadership approval and Board determination.

ARTICLE XII

PRINCIPLE OF READINESS AND FINAL AUTHORITY

Section 11.1 — Readiness for Advancement

Each promotion within TKKC shall reflect not only completion of coursework, but also spiritual maturity, discipline, reliability, fellowship, and readiness to bear greater responsibility within the Order.

Section 11.2 — Final Authority

All promotion decisions remain subject to the final authority of TKKC leadership and the Board of Promotion.

Section 11.3 — Implementation

These By-Laws shall be implemented as part of the official Order Manual and shall govern the promotion process upon admission of members into the Order.
