Knight Lieutenant Handbook

Purpose of the Academy and Knight Lieutenant Handbook

This handbook establishes the curriculum and operational guidelines for the Knight program of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ - Templar Forge Academy. The Academy is strictly educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose. The Knight program represents the pinnacle of formation within the Order, where members are examined for solemn knightly responsibility and inward spiritual consecration.

It must be explicitly stated that the Order and its Academy are not military or paramilitary in nature. All internal ranks, including that of Knight, are organizational and instructional only. The program exists to forge individuals of strong moral character dedicated to charitable works and rigorous spiritual formation, completely devoid of martial posturing or unofficial military style.

Mission and Historical Foundation

The primary mission of the Templar Forge Academy is to cultivate a truthful understanding of the medieval Knights Templar, rejecting conjecture and myth in favor of genuine historical appreciation and spiritual emulation. The Order is forever consecrated to Jesus Christ, and this dedication shapes our modern mission to provide a truthful, nonviolent witness to His gospel through sacrificial service and brotherhood.

The historical foundation of the Knight program delves deeply into the monastic roots of the original Templar Order. It demands that a true knight must first be a monk before he is a warrior, rooted in prayer, Scripture, humility, and obedience before engaging in the spiritual warfare of the modern era.

Core Principles

The Knight program requires strict adherence to the core principles that govern the conduct, study, and daily lives of our members. Candidates are trained to embody:

  • Truth before legend: Committing to rigorous, source-based historical analysis over popular, romanticized fabrications.
  • Service before status: Embracing the principle of service before self, viewing advancement as a call to heavier burdens, not greater privileges.
  • Discipline before display: Emphasizing internal spiritual mastery, strict conduct, and obedience over outward organizational pageantry.
  • Charity before privilege: Ensuring that the resources, influence, and efforts of the Order are directed toward those in genuine need.
  • Leadership through accountability: Recognizing that no member is above reproach, and all are accountable to God, Scripture, and the fellowship of the Order. Leaders are held to a higher standard of visible virtue and humility.

Program Audience and Scope

The Knight program is an adult formation program specifically designed for members who have successfully completed the prior required formation stages. It transitions the candidate from academic and logistical study into the deepest levels of spiritual discipline, preparing them for solemn vows of knightly responsibility and selfless service.

Program Standards and Legitimacy Measures

Institutional integrity is guarded through strict adherence to established program standards. The Academy utilizes signed enrollment records, direct Schoolmaster oversight, written course standards, formal rank boards, and an annual curriculum review to maintain the highest quality of formation. Reading requirements and mandatory service work are integrated directly into the curriculum.

The Templar Forge Academy enforces a clear and absolute rejection of false lineage claims, invented authority, theatrical succession claims, and any unofficial military style. The Order is educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose, and not military or paramilitary in nature. Internal ranks are organizational and instructional only.

Student Expectations and Records Maintained

Candidates in the Knight program are expected to maintain the highest standards of participation, rigorous study, and selfless service. To ensure these standards are met, the Academy requires the meticulous maintenance of the following records:

  • Attendance logs
  • Reading logs
  • Service logs
  • Assessment sheets
  • Oral-board notes
  • Advancement recommendations

Assessment Model

Throughout the 4-week program, candidates are evaluated based on five foundational pillars of assessment:

  • Knowledge: Deep understanding of the monastic foundation, spiritual warfare, and servant leadership.
  • Judgment: The exercise of mature Christian discernment, restraint, and the ability to protect the vulnerable.
  • Service: Active, documented participation in the charitable works and stewardship of the Order, demonstrating service before self.
  • Character: Consistent demonstration of rigorous discipline, brotherhood, humility, and moral integrity.
  • Communication: The ability to articulate truth with honor, mercy, and justice in all interactions.

Full 4-Week Knight Curriculum

Week 1: The Calling of a Knight

Primary Focus

Monk before warrior; inward consecration before outward rank; surrender of personal ambition; monastic foundation of the original Templar Order; hidden devotion and private prayer; dying to self in order to live for Christ.

Reflection Questions

Where are you seeking outward recognition over inward purity?

What does "monk before warrior" demand of you personally?

Discussion Questions

Why is a warrior without the discipline of a monk dangerous to the Order and the Church?

How do we maintain inward consecration in a culture obsessed with outward success?

Readiness Goal

Demonstrate a sincere surrender of personal ambition and an embrace of hidden, inward devotion.

Week 2: Rule of Life

Week 2: Rule of Life

Primary Focus

Prayer, Scripture, obedience, discipline, accountability, self-mastery; establishing a daily rhythm of prayer and Scripture; obedience to legitimate authority; self-mastery over appetites, time, and desires; transparent accountability.

Reflection Questions

Where does your current daily routine lack spiritual discipline?

How do you respond internally to an order or directive you dislike?

Discussion Questions

How does a structured Rule of Life create spiritual freedom rather than legalism?

What role does accountability play in preventing moral failure in leadership?

Readiness Goal

Establish and articulate a rigorous personal Rule of Life anchored in daily spiritual discipline.

Week 3: Virtue in Action

Primary Focus

Charity, protection of the weak, honorable speech, courage with restraint, mercy, justice, and brotherhood; speaking truth with honor; power restrained; actively protecting the vulnerable.

Reflection Questions

When is restraint more courageous for you than taking action?

Does your speech consistently reflect honor, truth, and charity?

Discussion Questions

How do we practically protect the weak and vulnerable in modern society?

Why is genuine brotherly unity essential for the spiritual effectiveness of the Order?

Readiness Goal

Exhibit actionable charity, restraint of power, and honorable speech in all daily interactions.

Week 4: Knightly Witness and Readiness

Primary Focus

Spiritual warfare, steadfastness, service, leadership through humility, final readiness for knightly responsibility; putting on the full armor of God; servant leadership; steadfastness in trial; final self-examination.

Reflection Questions

Are you prepared to accept the burdens of leadership without the praises of men?

What vulnerabilities currently exist in your spiritual armor?

Discussion Questions

What does it practically mean to engage in spiritual warfare today?

How will you ensure that promotion to Knight results in greater service rather than greater pride?

Readiness Goal

Complete final self-examination confirming absolute readiness for solemn knightly vows and selfless service.

Optional Supplemental Reading

The following readings are entirely optional, non-tested, and provided solely for the personal spiritual enrichment and deeper formation of the candidate during the 4-week program:

  • Week 1: In Praise of the New Knighthood by Bernard of Clairvaux; The Rule of Saint Benedict
  • Week 2: The Practice of the Presence of God by Brother Lawrence; Celebration of Discipline by Richard J. Foster
  • Week 3: Mere Christianity by C.S. Lewis; The Imitation of Christ by Thomas à Kempis
  • Week 4: Life Together by Dietrich Bonhoeffer; The Pilgrim’s Progress by John Bunyan

Schoolmaster Guidance

Promotion to the rank of Knight is a solemn trust, not an automatic right or a mere acknowledgment of academic completion. As Schoolmasters and mentors, you are tasked with observing the inward character and outward conduct of the aspirant. Look for genuine humility, a steadfast prayer life, and a servant's heart.

It must be stated plainly: an aspirant who knows all the answers but lacks charity, obedience, and discipline is not ready to bear the cross of knighthood. Guide them with firm love, holding them to the highest standard of Christian chivalry and spiritual maturity.

Promotion Readiness Statement

Completion of the 4-week Knight curriculum is strictly required but does not guarantee automatic promotion. The Board of Promotion Review will conduct a profound evaluation of the candidate. This evaluation considers coursework completion, logged attendance, active

participation, selfless service, disciplined conduct, oral readiness, and overall conduct becoming of a member.

It is an unyielding principle of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ that ranks cannot be bought or sold. Advancement is earned solely through inward character, tested virtue, and an ongoing dedication to the charitable mission of the Order.

Suggested Annual Academy Cycle

To provide structure for this pinnacle stage of adult formation, the Knight program operates on a suggested annual cycle:

  • One major four-week Knight term for advancing candidates.
  • One preparation and review term dedicated to deep spiritual study and readiness.
  • One retreat or spiritual formation intensive focusing on prayer and brotherhood.
  • One annual service campaign led by Knights in a spirit of servant leadership.
  • One solemn recognition ceremony to formally elevate those who have accepted the vows of service.

Closing Affirmation

The Knight Program of the Templar Forge Academy stands as a testament to our commitment to a truthful understanding of the medieval Knights Templar. We remain an organization that is strictly educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose, forever consecrated to Jesus Christ. We affirm that service before self is our highest calling, and we declare unconditionally that no superior can command an act violating Scripture or civil law. Those who achieve the rank of Knight, and those who lead them, are held to a higher standard of visible virtue and humility, ensuring that our Order remains a beacon of nonviolent, truthful witness. We are not military or paramilitary in nature; our internal ranks are organizational and instructional only, functioning solely to structure our pursuit of truth, charity, and Christ-like discipline.
