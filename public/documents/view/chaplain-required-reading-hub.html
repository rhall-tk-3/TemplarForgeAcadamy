✠ Specialist Corps Certification

Chaplain Corps
Required Reading Hub

A 6-week certification course grounded in the historic Templar chaplaincy. Study the history, the calling, and the living standards of spiritual leadership within the Order.

Duration 6 Weeks
Type Specialist Certification
Passing Score 70%
Root Document Rule of the Temple, 1129

Weekly Reading Schedule

Week 1
The Calling: Identity, Authority, and the Templar Chaplain
The history and identity of the Templar chaplain from 1129 to today; the KTKC Chaplain Corps mission; offices of Master and Assistant Chaplain; certification vs. ordination.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 1
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles I–II)
  • Chaplain Certification Syllabus — Unit 1
Reflection Prompt: Why is it significant that the original Knights Templar included chaplains as a formal class — not just an optional addition? What would the Order have lost without them?
Week 2
The Foundation: Christian Identity, Chivalric Conduct, and Templar Spiritual Standards
The explicitly Christian foundation of KTKC chaplaincy; the five core standards (Reverence, Charity, Confidentiality, Discipline of Speech, Christian Example); the historic-to-modern correlation standard.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 2
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles III, V)
  • Chaplain Instructor Edition — Units 2 & 3
Reflection Prompt: How does the Templar practice of structured daily prayer — Matins, canonical hours, Mass — shape the modern chaplain's understanding of prayer as a duty, not merely a personal devotion?
Week 3
The Voice: Public Prayer, Scripture Ministry, and Devotional Leadership
The five elements of formal prayer; leading invocations and benedictions; reading Scripture with authority and reverence; preparing and delivering a brief devotional.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 3
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles IV, VI)
  • Chaplain Certification Syllabus — Units 4 & 5
Reflection Prompt: The historic Templar chaplain was the Order's voice before God in community. Every conclave that opens with prayer today is performing the same act. What does it mean to carry that responsibility?
Week 4
The Shepherd: Pastoral Care, Visitation, and Ministry of Presence
Ministry of presence as the foundation of pastoral care; historic Templar hospitals and care for the dying; visitation SOPs 2–4; escalation thresholds and non-negotiable reporting obligations.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 4
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles IV, VII, XII SOPs 2–4)
  • Chaplain Student Handbook — Weeks 7 & 8
Reflection Prompt: The medieval Templar Order maintained hospitals and infirmaries because spiritual care in suffering is as important as spiritual care in celebration. How does this historic commitment shape the modern chaplain's approach to visitation?
Week 5
The Conscience: Ethics, Confidentiality, Boundaries, and Reporting
Chaplain confidentiality and its defined limits; defining and identifying spiritual abuse; pastoral boundaries; writing factual, non-speculative incident reports; non-interference after escalation.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 5
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles VII, XIII)
  • Chaplain Certification Syllabus — Units 9 & 10
  • Chaplain Exam Packet — Ethics Questions
Reflection Prompt: Why is it essential that confidentiality be a core chaplain standard — and why is it equally essential that it has defined limits? What would happen to the Order if those limits were ignored in either direction?
Week 6
The Commission: Ceremony, Observances, and Chaplain Readiness
The chaplain's role in KTKC ceremony; planning an order of service; capstone integration across all five core standards; the living legacy of the Templar chaplaincy; commissioning readiness and final assessment.
  • Chaplain Program Handbook — Week 6
  • KTKC Chaplain Core Manual (Articles XI, XIV — The Model Charge)
  • Chaplain Certification Syllabus — Units 11 & 12
  • Chaplain Exam Packet — Full Certification Exam
Capstone Reflection Prompt: Looking back over six weeks — how has studying the history of the Templar chaplain shaped how you understand your own calling? What will you do differently as a KTKC chaplain because of what you have learned?

✠ Key Discussion Topics — All Weeks

  • Week 1 · Identity Why is it significant that the original 1129 Rule of the Temple formally recognized chaplains as one of the three classes? What does this say about the spiritual foundation the Order was built on?
  • Week 2 · Foundation How did the historic Templars' daily rhythm of prayer — Matins, canonical hours, Mass — reflect the belief that an order of knights must first be an order of prayer? How does this apply to the KTKC today?
  • Week 3 · Prayer Ministry What is the difference between a prayer that opens a ceremony and one that transforms it? What qualities does the chaplain bring to formal prayer that no one else in the Order can?
  • Week 4 · Pastoral Care The Templar chaplain sat with the dying. The modern KTKC chaplain visits the hospitalized, the bereaved, and the inactive. What spiritual continuity runs through both of these ministries across nine centuries?
  • Week 5 · Ethics Spiritual abuse damages the most vulnerable members of a community. How does the chaplain's unique position — as the trusted spiritual officer — both make this harm possible and make the chaplain responsible to prevent it?
  • Week 6 · Commission What does it mean to carry the unbroken line of Templar chaplaincy from 1129 into the KTKC today? What have you committed to by completing this program?
