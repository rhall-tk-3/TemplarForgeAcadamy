Corporal School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
The Crusades: Context, Cause, and Consequence
  • ✦Understand the historical conditions that produced the Crusades
  • ✦Identify the major Crusades and their outcomes
  • ✦Assess the Templar role in the Crusader states
Week 2 of 8
The Grand Masters and Leadership of the Order
  • ✦Identify key Grand Masters of the Templar Order
  • ✦Analyze the leadership qualities that made or broke their tenure
  • ✦Apply lessons of Templar leadership to modern organizational life
Week 3 of 8
Templar Finance, Banking, and the Letters of Credit
  • ✦Understand the Templars' role in medieval banking
  • ✦Explain how the letters of credit system worked
  • ✦Connect Templar financial innovation to modern fiduciary responsibility
Week 4 of 8
The Rule of Benedict and the Monastic Influence on the Templars
  • ✦Understand the Rule of St. Benedict and its influence on Templar practice
  • ✦Identify how monastic discipline shaped the Templar character
  • ✦Distinguish between monastic vows and Templar vows
Week 5 of 8
Leadership, Delegation, and the Marshal System
  • ✦Understand the Templar military command structure
  • ✦Identify the role of the Marshal and Seneschal
  • ✦Apply principles of delegation and accountability to modern leadership
Week 6 of 8
The Fall of the Templars: Betrayal, Trial, and Legacy
  • ✦Understand the political circumstances of the Templar suppression
  • ✦Evaluate the charges brought against the Order
  • ✦Reflect on the legacy of the Templars and what it means for modern orders
Week 7 of 8
Conflict Resolution and the Templar Standard of Justice
  • ✦Understand how the medieval Templars handled internal discipline
  • ✦Apply principles of just conflict resolution to the modern Order
  • ✦Practice the skill of delivering and receiving correction with grace
Week 8 of 8
Corporal Advancement Review Preparation
  • ✦Review all major topics from the Corporal program
  • ✦Prepare to articulate the Corporal's role within the Order
  • ✦Identify one area of leadership growth to pursue in the Sergeant program

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
