Knight Captain School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 12-Week Program
Week 1 of 12
The Captain's Mandate: Leading Functional Areas
  • ✦Understand the Captain's expanded scope across functional areas
  • ✦Identify the functional areas typically under a Captain's authority
  • ✦Design a simple organizational chart for your area of responsibility
Week 2 of 12
Developing Officers: The Captain's Primary Investment
  • ✦Understand why developing other officers is the Captain's most important work
  • ✦Learn the officer development cycle
  • ✦Apply the cycle to two officers currently under your authority
Week 3 of 12
Strategic Planning for the Order's Future
  • ✦Understand the Captain's role in multi-year strategic planning
  • ✦Learn the components of a simple strategic plan
  • ✦Develop a three-year plan for one functional area
Week 4 of 12
Resource Management: People, Time, and Money
  • ✦Understand the Captain's responsibility for all three forms of resource
  • ✦Apply allocation principles to a specific planning scenario
  • ✦Identify the most common resource mismanagement patterns in voluntary organizations
Week 5 of 12
Inter-Officer Relations: Collaboration Across Functional Areas
  • ✦Understand the Captain's obligation to collaborate with peer officers
  • ✦Learn the common causes of inter-officer conflict
  • ✦Practice the skill of peer-level communication and cooperation
Week 6 of 12
Crisis Communication and Reputation Management
  • ✦Understand how crises damage organizational reputation if mishandled
  • ✦Learn the principles of honest, timely crisis communication
  • ✦Practice preparing a crisis response statement
Week 7 of 12
Theological Foundations of Leadership Authority
  • ✦Study the Scriptural basis for servant authority
  • ✦Apply the theology of servant leadership to the Captain's role
  • ✦Reflect on the gap between stated values and actual practice
Week 8 of 12
Building a Culture of Excellence
  • ✦Define excellence as a standard, not an event
  • ✦Identify the conditions that produce excellent cultures
  • ✦Apply the conditions framework to your functional area
Week 9 of 12
Navigating Organizational Politics with Integrity
  • ✦Understand the reality of organizational politics in any voluntary association
  • ✦Learn to navigate politics without becoming cynical or corrupt
  • ✦Apply the integrity framework to a specific political challenge
Week 10 of 12
The Captain and the Community: Building External Partnerships
  • ✦Understand the Captain's role in building relationships outside the Order
  • ✦Identify potential community partners and the principles of healthy partnership
  • ✦Draft a proposal for one specific community partnership
Week 11 of 12
Preparing for Commander-Level Responsibility
  • ✦Understand the scope of the Commander's role
  • ✦Identify the gaps in your current competency that the Major program will address
  • ✦Prepare a realistic assessment of your readiness for further advancement
Week 12 of 12
Captain Review and Major Program Preparation
  • ✦Review all twelve weeks of the Captain program
  • ✦Prepare a comprehensive senior officer portfolio
  • ✦Set clear, specific goals for the Major program

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
