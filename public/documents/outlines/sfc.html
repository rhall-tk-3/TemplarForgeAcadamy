Sergeant First Class School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
Systems Thinking: Building Organizations That Last
  • ✦Define systems thinking and why it matters in an Order
  • ✦Identify the key systems that sustain a functioning assembly
  • ✦Diagnose a weakness in the current assembly's systems
Week 2 of 8
Standards, Expectations, and Performance Culture
  • ✦Define a performance culture and why it matters
  • ✦Understand how clear standards prevent most discipline problems
  • ✦Practice communicating expectations clearly and kindly
Week 3 of 8
Succession Planning and Member Development
  • ✦Understand why succession planning is a moral obligation of rank
  • ✦Identify the members most ready for advancement
  • ✦Create a simple development plan for one member
Week 4 of 8
Finances and Stewardship in the Order
  • ✦Understand the basic financial obligations of the Order
  • ✦Apply the Templar standard of fiduciary care to modern Order finances
  • ✦Identify the three principles of sound organizational stewardship
Week 5 of 8
Crisis Leadership: Steady in the Storm
  • ✦Understand what distinguishes crisis leadership from normal leadership
  • ✦Learn the four principles of calm command under pressure
  • ✦Apply these principles to a scenario from Order or personal life
Week 6 of 8
Communication: Written, Verbal, and Ceremonial
  • ✦Understand the three registers of communication in the Order
  • ✦Learn the standards for formal written and verbal communication
  • ✦Practice delivering a brief formal address or report
Week 7 of 8
Honor, Reputation, and the Long Game
  • ✦Understand honor as a long-term investment, not a short-term display
  • ✦Identify behaviors that build or destroy a member's reputation over time
  • ✦Commit to one specific long-term practice of honor
Week 8 of 8
SFC Advancement Review Preparation
  • ✦Review the full scope of the Sergeant First Class program
  • ✦Prepare to demonstrate systems thinking and leadership maturity
  • ✦Set a clear goal for the Knight Aspirant program

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
