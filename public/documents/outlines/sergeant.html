Sergeant School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
Command Authority and the Duty to Lead
  • ✦Define command authority in the context of the Order
  • ✦Understand the difference between authority given and authority earned
  • ✦Identify the responsibilities that accompany a leadership role
Week 2 of 8
Mentorship and the Formation of Younger Members
  • ✦Understand the Sergeant's obligation to mentor Levies and Corporals
  • ✦Learn effective practices for one-on-one mentorship
  • ✦Reflect on who has mentored you and what that has meant
Week 3 of 8
Assembly Management and Formal Procedure
  • ✦Understand the proper structure of a formal assembly
  • ✦Learn the Sergeant's role in maintaining order during assembly
  • ✦Identify common procedural errors and how to prevent them
Week 4 of 8
Templar Spirituality: Interior Conversion and Outward Conduct
  • ✦Understand the distinction between interior and exterior religion
  • ✦Study the Templar tradition of spiritual formation beyond the Rule
  • ✦Develop a personal practice of interior formation
Week 5 of 8
Community Engagement and the Order's Public Mission
  • ✦Understand the Order's obligations to the surrounding community
  • ✦Identify specific community service opportunities
  • ✦Plan and commit to a specific service project
Week 6 of 8
Discipline Without Harshness: Correcting Members Effectively
  • ✦Understand the Sergeant's role in member discipline
  • ✦Learn the distinction between correction and condemnation
  • ✦Practice giving correction that is clear, kind, and effective
Week 7 of 8
Strategic Thinking and the Art of Forward Planning
  • ✦Understand why strategic thinking is a Sergeant's responsibility
  • ✦Learn the three horizons of planning: immediate, seasonal, and long-range
  • ✦Apply strategic thinking to one aspect of Order or personal life
Week 8 of 8
Sergeant Advancement Review Preparation
  • ✦Review the full scope of the Sergeant program
  • ✦Prepare to articulate the Sergeant's unique contribution
  • ✦Set specific goals for the Sergeant First Class program

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
