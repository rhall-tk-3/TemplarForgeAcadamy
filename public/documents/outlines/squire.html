Squire School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
The History of the Knights Templar
  • ✦Understand the founding of the Knights Templar in 1118 AD
  • ✦Identify the nine founding knights and their mission
  • ✦Explain the original purpose of protecting Christian pilgrims
Week 2 of 8
The Code of Honor and Chivalry
  • ✦Define chivalry and its key virtues
  • ✦Identify the seven knightly virtues
  • ✦Apply principles of chivalry to daily conduct
Week 3 of 8
The Structure of the Order
  • ✦Understand the rank structure of the KTKC
  • ✦Identify the role of each rank from Levie through Commander
  • ✦Explain the purpose of the Schoolmaster and program progression
Week 4 of 8
Prayer, Scripture, and the Templar Faith
  • ✦Understand the role of faith in the Templar tradition
  • ✦Learn the Order's opening and closing prayers
  • ✦Reflect on Scripture passages used in Templar ceremony
Week 5 of 8
Service, Duty, and the Squire's Pledge
  • ✦Define service as a core obligation of membership
  • ✦Understand what is meant by 'duty before self'
  • ✦Prepare to articulate the Squire's Pledge with sincerity
Week 6 of 8
Symbols, Regalia, and Ceremony
  • ✦Identify the key symbols used by the Templar Order
  • ✦Understand the meaning of the cross, the mantle, and the seal
  • ✦Explain the purpose and reverence of formal ceremony
Week 7 of 8
Brotherhood, Loyalty, and the Bond of the Order
  • ✦Define brotherhood as it applies to the Order
  • ✦Understand the obligations members have to one another
  • ✦Identify conduct that strengthens or weakens the bonds of brotherhood
Week 8 of 8
Advancement, Examination, and the Path Forward
  • ✦Review all major topics covered in the Squire program
  • ✦Demonstrate readiness for formal advancement review
  • ✦Articulate personal goals for continued growth in the Order

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
