Knight Aspirant School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
The Meaning and Obligation of Knighthood
  • ✦Define knighthood as a vocation, not merely a rank
  • ✦Understand the historical obligations of the medieval knight
  • ✦Articulate a personal vision of what knighthood demands from you
Week 2 of 8
The Knighting Ceremony: History and Meaning
  • ✦Understand the history of the dubbing ceremony
  • ✦Identify each element of the knighting ritual and its symbolism
  • ✦Prepare yourself spiritually and personally for the ceremony ahead
Week 3 of 8
The Beatitudes and Knightly Virtue
  • ✦Study the Beatitudes of Matthew 5 as a framework for knightly character
  • ✦Connect each Beatitude to a specific knightly virtue or practice
  • ✦Identify the Beatitude most challenging for you personally
Week 4 of 8
Templar Combat Ethics and the Just War Tradition
  • ✦Understand the Christian Just War tradition as it applies to Templar service
  • ✦Identify the criteria for a just conflict
  • ✦Apply just war principles to modern situations of conflict
Week 5 of 8
Contemplation and Action: The Dual Life of the Templar Knight
  • ✦Understand the tension between contemplative and active life
  • ✦Learn how the Templars integrated both in their Rule
  • ✦Develop a personal rhythm of prayer, study, and action
Week 6 of 8
The Knight's Oath: Understanding What You Are Committing To
  • ✦Study the structure and content of the knightly oath
  • ✦Understand the gravity of oath-taking in the Templar tradition
  • ✦Prepare to take the oath with full knowledge and sincere intent
Week 7 of 8
Serving the Vulnerable: The Knight's Primary Duty
  • ✦Understand the Templars' historic commitment to the poor and helpless
  • ✦Identify specific vulnerable populations the Order can serve today
  • ✦Commit to a sustained service relationship with one such group
Week 8 of 8
Knight Aspirant Final Review and Ceremony Preparation
  • ✦Review all major topics from the Knight Aspirant program
  • ✦Prepare personally and spiritually for the knighting ceremony
  • ✦Write a final statement of readiness and intent

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
