Levie School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 8-Week Program
Week 1 of 8
Origins and Mission of the Order
  • ✦Explain the historical founding of the Knights Templar
  • ✦Understand the KTKC's connection to that heritage
  • ✦State the mission of the modern Order in your own words
Week 2 of 8
The Seven Virtues and Daily Conduct
  • ✦Identify and define the seven knightly virtues
  • ✦Relate each virtue to a concrete behavior in daily life
  • ✦Evaluate your own areas of strength and growth
Week 3 of 8
The Rule of the Order and Obligations of Membership
  • ✦Understand what the Rule of the Templars required
  • ✦Identify the ongoing obligations of KTKC membership
  • ✦Distinguish between rules as restrictions and rules as framework for excellence
Week 4 of 8
Prayer, Scripture, and the Templar Liturgy
  • ✦Understand the role of daily prayer in Templar formation
  • ✦Learn key Scriptures referenced in the Order's tradition
  • ✦Reflect on the spiritual dimension of membership
Week 5 of 8
Service and the Obligation to Community
  • ✦Define service as the primary reason the Order exists
  • ✦Identify specific service opportunities within and outside the Order
  • ✦Understand how personal service builds the Order's reputation
Week 6 of 8
Symbols, Ceremony, and the Meaning of Regalia
  • ✦Identify key symbols of the Templar Order and their meanings
  • ✦Understand the purpose of formal ceremony in the Order's life
  • ✦Explain why regalia is treated with reverence
Week 7 of 8
Brotherhood, Accountability, and the Chain of Authority
  • ✦Understand brotherhood as covenant rather than casual friendship
  • ✦Explain the chain of authority from Levie to Grand Master
  • ✦Describe the practice of fraternal accountability
Week 8 of 8
Final Review and Advancement Preparation
  • ✦Review all core topics from the Levie program
  • ✦Prepare a brief personal statement of commitment
  • ✦Identify the next program in your advancement path

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
