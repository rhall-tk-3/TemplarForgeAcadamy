Knight Lieutenant School Program
Weekly Lesson Outline — 12-Week Program
Week 1 of 12
Introduction to Officer Formation: Authority and Accountability
  • ✦Define officer formation and how it differs from rank formation
  • ✦Understand the dual obligation of authority and accountability
  • ✦Identify the specific responsibilities of a Lieutenant in the Order
Week 2 of 12
The Officer's Relationship with Subordinates
  • ✦Understand the officer's obligation to serve those under their authority
  • ✦Practice the discipline of listening before directing
  • ✦Identify the three modes of officer engagement with subordinates
Week 3 of 12
Planning and Execution: The Officer's Operational Cycle
  • ✦Understand the four stages of the operational cycle
  • ✦Apply the cycle to planning a specific Order event or program
  • ✦Identify common points of failure in each stage
Week 4 of 12
Doctrine, Tradition, and the Officer's Role as Guardian
  • ✦Understand the difference between doctrine and tradition
  • ✦Identify which elements of the Order's practice are fixed and which are flexible
  • ✦Practice the skill of preserving essentials while allowing adaptation
Week 5 of 12
Conflict Management at the Officer Level
  • ✦Understand the officer's unique responsibility in conflict situations
  • ✦Learn the escalation ladder for handling conflict
  • ✦Practice de-escalation language and technique
Week 6 of 12
The Officer as Institutional Memory
  • ✦Understand the role of institutional memory in organizational health
  • ✦Identify practices that build and preserve institutional memory
  • ✦Begin maintaining records that serve the Order's future leaders
Week 7 of 12
Spiritual Formation of Officers: The Inner Life of Authority
  • ✦Understand why spiritual formation is more important, not less, at officer level
  • ✦Study the example of humility in authority from the Templar tradition
  • ✦Develop a personal practice of officer-level spiritual formation
Week 8 of 12
Public Communication and the Order's External Presence
  • ✦Understand the officer's role as the Order's public representative
  • ✦Learn the principles of effective external communication
  • ✦Practice preparing and delivering a public statement about the Order
Week 9 of 12
Leading Change: When and How to Reform
  • ✦Understand the difference between reform and disruption
  • ✦Learn the conditions under which change is appropriate
  • ✦Apply a change management framework to one aspect of Order life
Week 10 of 12
Ethics and the Officer: Navigating Difficult Decisions
  • ✦Understand the ethical framework for officer decision-making
  • ✦Apply the framework to three common officer dilemmas
  • ✦Develop a personal decision-making protocol for difficult situations
Week 11 of 12
Developing the Next Generation of Officers
  • ✦Understand the officer's responsibility for identifying and forming future officers
  • ✦Learn the difference between leadership development and task assignment
  • ✦Create a two-year development plan for one identified future officer
Week 12 of 12
Lieutenant Review and Captain Program Preparation
  • ✦Review the twelve weeks of officer formation at the Lieutenant level
  • ✦Prepare a comprehensive officer portfolio for advancement review
  • ✦Set clear goals for the Captain program

This outline lists weekly topics and learning objectives only. Detailed lesson content, key terms, and examinations are delivered through your in-app lesson viewer. Exam questions and answer keys are not included in this document.
