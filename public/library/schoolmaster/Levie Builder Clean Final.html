Program Purpose

The Levie School provides foundational entry-level formation for new members of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ. The first four weeks focus on rules, conduct, standards, duties, and Order identity. The final four weeks focus only on early history leading up to the beginning of the Templars, without moving into later-rank historical material. [Source](/documents/levie/levie-lesson-plan.docx)

How Reading Works

The updated Levie reading list divides the program into two halves: Weeks 1–4 for rules, standards, conduct, and identity; Weeks 5–8 for early pre-Templar and beginning-of-Templar history only. Reading should be completed weekly and recorded in the student reading log. [Source](/documents/levie/levie-required-reading-list.docx)

Student Reading Log

The Levie student reading log includes weekly checkbox tracking, instructor initials, notes and reflection areas, and a final completion summary so it can be used as a regular review tool throughout the probationary period. [Source](/documents/levie/levie-student-reading-log.docx)

Open Student Reading Log

How to Use It

  • Complete reading before the weekly lesson discussion.
  • Review the weekly memory targets.
  • Record notes, reflections, or questions.
  • Have the Schoolmaster initial after review.
  • Finish the final summary at the end of Week 8.

Parent Section

Parents can help by reviewing the weekly reading focus, helping students keep a steady reading rhythm, supporting note completion in the reading log, and encouraging honest and calm discussion rather than rushed memorization. [Source](/documents/levie/levie-required-reading-list.docx)

Parent Support Priorities

  • Review one weekly section at a time.
  • Help students connect reading to conduct and discipline.
  • Keep the historical section limited to the early background stage.
  • Use the reading log as the main weekly checkpoint.
