Member Guidance

Practical counsel for the Knight Aspirant as he prepares for Knight review.

Approach the program seriously

  • Keep a daily rhythm of prayer and Scripture.
  • Attend meetings and class sessions faithfully.
  • Maintain humility, sober speech, and charity.
  • Treat the essay as a witness of formation, not a formality.

Rule of life suggestions

  • Morning and evening prayer
  • Daily Scripture reading
  • Weekly self-examination and confession
  • Visible acts of charity and service
  • Respectful speech and disciplined conduct
A Knight Aspirant should resist fantasy, pride, and theatrical ideas of knighthood. The better question is not “How do I appear knightly?” but “How am I being conformed to Christ in discipline, virtue, and service?”

Essay preparation reminders

Keep returning to the central essay title: “What Does a Templar Knight Mean to You?” Answer it with spiritual seriousness, personal honesty, and clear connection to the virtues and discipline required by the Order.
