Week 8: Final Submission, Oral Readiness, and Review

Essay Phase · Submit the final essay by the assigned deadline and prepare to orally articulate core points to leadership. Stand ready for Board of Promotion review.

Week8

Key emphasis

  • Final essay submitted on deadline
  • Oral articulation readiness
  • Impeccable conduct during review period
  • Yield to the Board of Promotion

Primary focus

Submit the final essay by the assigned deadline and prepare to orally articulate the core points of the essay to leadership if asked.

Formation emphasis

Standing ready. The aspirant has done the work and now submits to the judgment and timing of the Order’s leadership.

Readiness goal

Submit the final essay and maintain impeccable conduct during the review period.

Scripture anchors

  • 1 Timothy 4:7–8
  • Micah 6:8

Required reading

  • Knight Aspirant Program Handbook — Section 14 (Promotion Readiness)
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws
  • KTKC Rules & Statutes
  • Women of the Knights Templar (supplemental, optional)

Reflection questions

  • Are you at peace with yielding your advancement entirely to the discernment of the Board of Promotion?
