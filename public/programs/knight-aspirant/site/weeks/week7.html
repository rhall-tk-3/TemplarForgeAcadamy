Week 7: Revision, Clarity, and Alignment with Templar Virtues

Essay Phase · Edit for grammar, flow, and formatting. Ensure correct cover page, single-spacing, and clear articulation of the “monk before warrior” concept.

Week7

Key emphasis

  • Grammar, flow, formatting
  • Cover page and single-spacing verified
  • “Monk before warrior” clearly expressed
  • Zero errors before final submission

Primary focus

Edit the essay for grammar, flow, and formatting. Ensure the cover page is correct, the document is single-spaced, and the essay clearly articulates the “monk before warrior” concept.

Formation emphasis

Discipline in the small things. Presenting a neat, well-edited document demonstrates respect for the Order and the process.

Readiness goal

Finalize the essay draft, completely free of errors.

Scripture anchors

  • Proverbs 15:1
  • Romans 12:18

Required reading

  • Knight Aspirant Program Handbook — Section 11 (Essay Specs)
  • Romans 12:18 (ESV)
  • Proverbs 15:1 (ESV)
  • 2 Timothy 2:23–24 (ESV)

Reflection questions

  • How does the discipline of revising your work mirror the discipline of spiritual repentance?
