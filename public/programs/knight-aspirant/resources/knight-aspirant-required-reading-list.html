Knight Aspirant Required Reading

Required reading hub for the Knight Aspirant formation cycle, organized by week and aligned to the lesson and essay schedule.

Essay title: "What Does a Templar Knight Mean to You?" · Standard college essay format · Cover page required · Single-spaced · One full page front and back, or two full typed pages in a Word document.

Program Orientation

The Knight Aspirant phase is a preparatory school of identity, discipline, prayer, virtue, and readiness. The early weeks establish the aspirant as a Christian man under rule before any claim to outward chivalric identity, while the later weeks move into essay development, reflection, and final review.

This page mirrors the academy hub format by organizing each week under its own lesson heading with required reading, formation focus, and readiness purpose.

Week 1 — The Calling of a Knight Aspirant

Primary Focus: Establish the identity of the Knight Aspirant as a Christian man under discipline, learning that a Templar must first become a monk before he can rightly stand as a warrior.

Key Formation Subjects: Christian identity under rule, submission to discipline, and the inward foundations of chivalric formation.

Formation Emphasis: Receiving the truth that spiritual formation must precede outward office, symbolism, or martial language.

Readiness Goal: Explain why a Knight Aspirant must first be formed as a disciplined Christian man before he can be considered ready for further advancement.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Lesson Plan Program
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws
  • KTKC Rules & Statutes

Week 2 — Prayer, Scripture, and Rule of Life

Primary Focus: Train the aspirant to build a daily rule of life grounded in prayer, Scripture, confession, sober speech, and steady accountability.

Key Formation Subjects: Daily prayer rhythm, Scripture intake, moral speech, confession, and practical accountability.

Formation Emphasis: Establishing stable habits that shape the aspirant from the inside rather than relying on occasional zeal.

Readiness Goal: Demonstrate understanding of a daily Christian rule of life and how it supports stability, obedience, and readiness for Knight formation.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Lesson Plan Program
  • KTKC Rules & Statutes
  • Britannica: Templars

Week 3 — Virtue, Charity, and Self-Mastery

Primary Focus: Focus on the virtues expected of a Christian Templar: charity, integrity, restraint, courage, mercy, honorable speech, and brotherly service.

Key Formation Subjects: Christian virtue, disciplined character, brotherly care, and mastery of appetite, tongue, and temper.

Formation Emphasis: Learning that maturity is proven in conduct, mercy, restraint, and service rather than appearance or intensity.

Readiness Goal: Identify the core virtues expected of a Knight Aspirant and describe how they should appear in ordinary chapter life.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Lesson Plan Program
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws
  • Britannica: Templars
  • OSMTH Modern Order History

Week 4 — Spiritual Warfare, Brotherhood, and Knight Readiness

Primary Focus: Clarify that the Knight's true warfare is first spiritual: resisting sin, guarding speech, protecting the weak, and standing faithfully within Christian brotherhood.

Key Formation Subjects: Spiritual warfare, disciplined speech, protection of the weak, and fidelity to Christian brotherhood.

Formation Emphasis: Reframing knightly readiness as spiritual steadiness, moral clarity, and faithful presence among the brethren.

Readiness Goal: Articulate the difference between outward romanticism and true Christian knightly readiness.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Lesson Plan Program
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws
  • KTKC Rules & Statutes

Week 5 — Essay Week I: Theme, Thesis, and Evidence

Primary Focus: Launch the required essay by identifying the central claim, spiritual theme, and supporting sources for "What Does a Templar Knight Mean to You?"

Key Formation Subjects: Theme selection, thesis development, source use, and disciplined reflection.

Formation Emphasis: Moving from vague aspiration to clear thought, grounded evidence, and sincere Christian reflection.

Readiness Goal: Produce a clear essay direction with an identifiable thesis and source base.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Required Reading List
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws
  • KTKC Rules & Statutes

Week 6 — Essay Week II: Outline and First Draft

Primary Focus: Build a complete outline and begin the full draft in standard college essay format, keeping the argument centered on Christian formation.

Key Formation Subjects: Structure, paragraph order, support, and disciplined drafting.

Formation Emphasis: Treating writing as an act of order, honesty, and careful self-expression rather than rushed opinion.

Readiness Goal: Present a workable outline and an honest first draft that reflects the theme of Christian knightly formation.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Required Reading List
  • Knight Aspirant Lesson Plan Program
  • Britannica: Templars

Week 7 — Essay Week III: Revision, Clarity, and Submission Readiness

Primary Focus: Revise for clarity, strength, sincerity, and discipline so the essay reflects maturity, not haste or sentimentality.

Key Formation Subjects: Revision, clarity, tone, honesty, and removal of weak or sentimental argument.

Formation Emphasis: Learning that mature speech and writing require restraint, refinement, and truthful self-examination.

Readiness Goal: Submit a revised draft that is orderly, clear, and fitting for final review.

Required Reading

  • Knight Aspirant Required Reading List
  • Promotion Manual & By-Laws

Week 8 — Essay Week IV: Final Submission and Knight Review

Primary Focus: Submit the final essay with cover page and formatting requirements completed, then undergo final review for Knight readiness.

Key Formation Subjects: Final compliance, submission readiness, review standards, and disciplined completion.

Formation Emphasis: Finishing well, honoring requirements, and presenting oneself for review with humility and seriousness.

Readiness Goal: Complete the Knight Aspirant formation cycle with the required essay and supporting reading record fully prepared for final review.
