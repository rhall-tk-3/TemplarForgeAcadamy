Member Guidance

The Knight program is meant to be practical and steady. A Knight should approach the course with prayer, honesty, and a visible desire to serve well inside the Order and outside it.

  • Keep the reading simple and consistent.
  • Meet regularly with your mentor or Schoolmaster.
  • Choose a Day of Service project that is realistic and Christ-honoring.
  • Let leadership begin with conduct before recognition.

Simple habits for the Knight

  • Short prayer partner check-in each week
  • Brief Scripture reflection after reading
  • One testimony or encouragement moment
  • Support a fellowship meal or church/community service effort

These light practices keep the course Christian, practical, and enjoyable without making it burdensome.
