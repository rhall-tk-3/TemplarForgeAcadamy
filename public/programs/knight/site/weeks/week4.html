Week 4

Day of Service and Leadership in Action

Week 4 puts leadership into action through planning or completing a Day of Service project rooted in Christian charity and public witness.

Key emphasis

  • Service as leadership
  • Planning a Christian project
  • Proof and accountability
  • Finishing the course with action

Reflection questions

  • What kind of service best reflects my local Christian witness?
  • Why did I choose this project or event?
  • How can service remain joyful, simple, and faithful?

Discussion questions

  • Why should a Knight lead through visible Christian service?
  • How does a Day of Service project reveal real leadership?

Readiness goal

Complete or clearly plan a Day of Service project with simple proof and mentor review.
