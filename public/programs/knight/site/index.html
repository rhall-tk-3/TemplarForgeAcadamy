Core Theme

Leadership Under Christ

Christian leadership inside KTKC and modern society.

Program Purpose

The Knight School prepares the knighted member to begin leadership with Christian humility, dependable conduct, and visible service. It stresses that Knightly rank must be expressed through prayer, Scripture, fellowship, responsibility, and charitable public witness rather than title alone.

Week-by-Week Path

The Knight course moves from inward leadership under Christ to outward service and witness.

Open Week 4

Day of Service requirement

Each Knight should complete at least one Day of Service project within the first year as a knighted Templar. The project may be joined or led, but it must clearly reflect Christian values and service-minded leadership.

  • Photos are encouraged but not required.
  • A church or event letter is encouraged.
  • Social platform posts may be accepted as proof.
  • If you lead the event, explain the details and why you chose it.
