Major Required Reading List

Officer Formation Level III

This concise required reading list is aligned to Officer Formation Level III and the Major School syllabus. It is designed to support the transition from local command to wider oversight, equipping officers with the foundational knowledge required for regional stewardship, systematic inspection, captain development, cross-commandery communication, accountability, and practicum readiness within the KTKC.

Weekly Reading Schedule

Week 1: The Burden of Wider Oversight

Focus: Understanding the transition from local command to regional stewardship and the burden of multiple bodies.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Review Course Purpose and Learning Objectives)
  • OSMTH Templarism and History

Week 2: The Provincial Structure of the Templars

Focus: Examining the historical division of the Order into provinces and the mechanics of wide-scale organization.

  • Knights Templar Overview (Wikipedia) (Focus on Organization and Structure)
  • Medieval Warfare: Templars Hierarchy and Duties

Week 3: The Office of Provincial Master / Grand Prior

Focus: The historical role, administrative authority, and operational duties of the Provincial Master.

  • Medieval Warfare: Templars Hierarchy and Duties (Focus on Provincial Master / Grand Prior)
  • Templars Overview (Britannica)

Week 4: Mission Alignment Across Multiple Bodies

Focus: Unifying diverse local commanderies under a single operational standard and core mission.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Mission Alignment)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Fordham) (Foundational unity and obedience)

Week 5: Inspection, Accountability, and Standards

Focus: Establishing accountability, maintaining strict standards, and conducting regional inspections.

  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Fordham) (Rules governing conduct and accountability)
  • OSMTH Modern Order History

Week 6: Recruitment, Growth, and Officer Pipeline

Focus: Sustaining the Order through strategic recruitment and building a capable pipeline of future officers.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Recruitment and Officer Pipeline)
  • OSMTH Templarism and History

Week 7: Resource Stewardship and Regional Planning

Focus: Managing regional assets, prioritizing resources, and planning for the long-term health of multiple bodies.

  • Knights Templar Overview (Wikipedia) (Focus on banking, assets, and regional estates)
  • OSMTH Modern Order History

Week 8: Training Captains and Strengthening Local Command

Focus: Mentoring local Captains to ensure their commanderies are strong, independent, and mission-capable.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Training Captains)
  • Medieval Warfare: Templars Hierarchy and Duties (Relationship between Preceptor and Provincial Master)

Week 9: Communication, Coordination, and Order

Focus: Developing clear protocols for region-wide communication, reporting, and logistical coordination.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Communication Protocols)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Fordham) (Order and clear hierarchy)

Week 10: Correction, Intervention, and Restoration at the Regional Level

Focus: Handling disorder appropriately, intervening in failing local bodies, and guiding restoration.

  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Fordham) (Penances, discipline, and correction)
  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Correction and Intervention)

Week 11: Regional Oversight Practicum

Focus: Applying regional oversight principles through a structured, real-world assessment or development project.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Practicum Expectations and AAR)

Week 12: Capstone Preparation and Major’s Board Review

Focus: Compiling the final portfolio, finalizing reflections, and preparing for the oral board evaluation.

  • Major School Syllabus PDF (Final Portfolio and Oral Board Expectations)
  • OSMTH Modern Order History (Reviewing the continuing legacy and responsibility)

Practical Written Work and Leadership Use

  • Reflection Papers & Assessments: Historical readings directly inform the required comparison papers and the local-body assessment template.
  • Regional Planning: Understanding the Templar provincial structure aids in drafting the 6-month regional growth plan and the regional priority map.
  • Captain Coaching & Communication: Principles of rule and order support the structured leadership review with a Captain and the development of a regional communication protocol.
  • Practicum & Portfolio: The syllabus and historical context guide the execution and After-Action Review (AAR) of the regional oversight practicum.
  • Oral Board: Mastery of the assigned reading is expected during the final review to demonstrate officer presence, judgment, historical grounding, and readiness for regional-level office.
