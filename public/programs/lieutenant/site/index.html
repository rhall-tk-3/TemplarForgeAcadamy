Officer Formation Level I

Lieutenant School

Lieutenant School forms the officer who can be trusted under authority. It marks the transition from committed member to dependable officer: a man who can receive mission, execute it faithfully, care for those assigned to him, maintain standards, and represent the Order with humility and steadiness. The historical model is the Templar Under Marshal, the clearest junior operational officer in the medieval hierarchy. [Source](/library/programs/lieutenant/lieutenant_syllabus.pdf)

Officer Model

Under Marshal

Final Standards

Portfolio + Oral Board

Course completion includes written work, practicum, portfolio, and officer review. [Source](/library/programs/lieutenant/lieutenant_syllabus.pdf)

Course purpose

The course develops a first-level officer who can lead under authority without ego, care for assigned brothers, execute direction clearly, and multiply standards through conduct, discipline, and reliability. It emphasizes obedience, readiness, disciplined speech, self-control, and fraternal care. [Source](/library/programs/lieutenant/lieutenant_syllabus.pdf)
