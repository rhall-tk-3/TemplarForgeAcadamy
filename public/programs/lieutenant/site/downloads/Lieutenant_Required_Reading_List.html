Lieutenant Program - Required Reading Hub

Officer Formation Level I — A 12-week reading progression for transitioning members into junior officers, modeled after the historical Templar Under Marshal.

This hub page organizes the Lieutenant reading cycle by week, aligning each lesson with its assigned sources, officer-formation emphasis, and readiness aim.

Program Orientation

The Lieutenant phase marks the transition into Officer Formation Level I. At this level, the member is no longer being formed only for personal stability, but for dependable service, lawful execution, disciplined communication, and practical care of other people.

The structure of this page mirrors the fuller academy hub format: weekly overview first, then detailed lesson sections with primary focus, formation themes, readiness goals, and correctly placed required reading links.

Week 1: The Call to Office

Primary Focus: Office as service, not status.

Key Formation Subjects: Humble service, obedience, motives for office, and the rejection of status-seeking.

Formation Emphasis: Receiving office as a burden of stewardship rather than a reward of prestige.

Readiness Goal: Clearly explain why a Lieutenant must view office as service under authority rather than personal elevation.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 1)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Sections on obedience and humble service)

Week 2: Origins of the Knights Templar

Primary Focus: Founding story and mission.

Key Formation Subjects: Founding need, original mission, protective service, and the identity of the early Order.

Formation Emphasis: Understanding that officer identity must remain tied to mission rather than symbolism alone.

Readiness Goal: Summarize the historical origins of the Templars and connect their founding mission to modern officer seriousness.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 2)
  • Knights Templar - Wikipedia (History & Origins)

Week 3: The Templar Chain of Command

Primary Focus: Hierarchy and Unity of Command.

Key Formation Subjects: Ordered structure, delegated authority, command relationships, and organizational coherence.

Formation Emphasis: Valuing lawful order and unity of command as safeguards against confusion and disorder.

Readiness Goal: Accurately describe the importance of hierarchy and explain why junior officers must support clarity in command.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 3)
  • Monastic Orders: Knights Templar (Hierarchy & Organization)

Week 4: The Under Marshal and the Lieutenant Model

Primary Focus: The Under Marshal as the clearest Templar parallel to a Junior Operational Officer.

Key Formation Subjects: Execution, readiness support, practical supervision, and operational dependability.

Formation Emphasis: Seeing lieutenant-level leadership as concrete service in support of mission and command.

Readiness Goal: Compare the Under Marshal model with lieutenant-level responsibilities and identify practical leadership parallels.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 4)
  • Monastic Orders: Knights Templar (Duties of the Under Marshal)

Week 5: The Rule: Obedience and Mission

Primary Focus: Obedience as a core virtue and submission to lawful authority.

Key Formation Subjects: Obedience, mission focus, lawful authority, and disciplined submission.

Formation Emphasis: Recovering obedience as strength of character rather than weakness of personality.

Readiness Goal: Demonstrate understanding of why obedience is essential to mission integrity and officer reliability.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 5)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Regulations on obedience)

Week 6: The Rule: Humility, Speech, and Bearing

Primary Focus: Self-command, humility, restraint, and disciplined speech.

Key Formation Subjects: Bearing, speech, restraint, humility, and visible self-command.

Formation Emphasis: Understanding that officer credibility is shaped as much by tone and bearing as by formal authority.

Readiness Goal: Show how disciplined speech and humble bearing directly affect the trustworthiness of a junior officer.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 6)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Regulations on conduct, speech, and bearing)

Week 7: Care of Brothers and Fraternal Responsibility

Primary Focus: Moral responsibility for people and caring for the weak.

Key Formation Subjects: Fraternal care, responsibility for others, concern for the vulnerable, and moral attentiveness.

Formation Emphasis: Learning that real officers do not merely direct tasks; they help carry people.

Readiness Goal: Explain how lieutenant-level office includes concrete moral responsibility for the welfare of brothers.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 7)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Care for the sick and aged)

Week 8: Readiness, Stewardship, and Reliability

Primary Focus: Operational preparedness and maintaining readiness.

Key Formation Subjects: Reliability, stewardship of duties, preparedness, and consistency in assigned responsibility.

Formation Emphasis: Internalizing that readiness is not accidental but maintained through ordered habits and vigilance.

Readiness Goal: Describe how a Lieutenant maintains practical readiness and dependable follow-through in ordinary operations.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 8)
  • Knights Templar - Wikipedia (Daily Life and Military Operations)

Week 9: Leading a Small Team

Primary Focus: Briefing, assigning, supervising, and accountability.

Key Formation Subjects: Briefing, delegation, supervision, follow-up, and team-level accountability.

Formation Emphasis: Moving from individual reliability into responsible oversight of a small unit or task team.

Readiness Goal: Demonstrate the ability to explain how a Lieutenant should brief, assign, supervise, and review limited responsibilities.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 9)
  • Monastic Orders: Knights Templar (Squadron deployment & duties)

Week 10: Correction, Conflict, and Discipline

Primary Focus: Mature problem solving and handling issues directly.

Key Formation Subjects: Correction, discipline, direct engagement, and mature conflict handling.

Formation Emphasis: Building the courage to address problems lawfully, calmly, and without avoidance.

Readiness Goal: Explain how a Lieutenant should approach conflict and correction with firmness, maturity, and procedural fairness.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 10)
  • Primitive Rule of the Templars (Discipline and Penances)

Week 11: Lieutenant as Mentor and Example

Primary Focus: Junior officers shape culture by example and multiplication.

Key Formation Subjects: Example, mentoring, multiplication of culture, and visible leadership influence.

Formation Emphasis: Recognizing that the habits of a junior officer are quietly reproduced in those who follow him.

Readiness Goal: Describe how a Lieutenant teaches not only by instruction but by consistent example and mentorship.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 11)
  • Knights Templar - Wikipedia (Legacy & Organizational Culture)

Week 12: Capstone Preparation and Officer Review

Primary Focus: Integration of learning and final portfolio submission.

Key Formation Subjects: Review, integration, documentation, final readiness, and officer-level accountability.

Formation Emphasis: Finishing the course with order, completeness, and sober readiness for evaluation.

Readiness Goal: Present a complete and integrated record of learning that demonstrates readiness for Lieutenant review.

Required Reading

  • Lieutenant School Syllabus PDF (Lesson 12 & Final Review Requirements)
