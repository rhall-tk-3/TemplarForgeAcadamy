Weeks 1–8 Lesson Pages

Browse the full Squire School sequence week by week. Each page includes lesson themes, reading focus, key figures, memory targets, and readiness goals drawn from the official curriculum.

Week 1

Origins and Virtues

Week 1 introduces the First Crusade, the importance of Jerusalem to medieval Christians, the danger faced by pilgrims, the reason an order was formed to protect them, and the core virtues expected of a Squire: honesty, courage, obedience, charity, and reverence.

Founding People and Places

Week 2 focuses on the founder most closely linked to the order, the king associated with the early setting in Jerusalem, the Temple Mount headquarters, and the crucial distinction between fact and legend.

Week 3

Approval and Mission

Week 3 teaches how the order gained formal support, how Bernard of Clairvaux is linked with encouragement of the order, what makes a religious order different from an ordinary army, and the early memory timeline from 1095 to 1139.

Week 4

Structure and Daily Life

Week 4 introduces the classes within the order, the major offices, the meaning of clothing and symbols, and the discipline of service before self.

Week 5

Life of the Order

Week 6

Power and Crisis

Week 6 explains why the Templars became powerful, how wealth and influence could create enemies, why Acre matters, and what mission drift means.

Week 7

Suppression and Courage

Week 7 teaches the arrests ordered in France in 1307, the suppression of the order in 1312, the final place of Jacques de Molay in the timeline, and the moral themes of justice, false accusation, courage, and endurance.

Review and Board Preparation

Week 8 brings the full course together through timeline review, map work, figure identification, service project presentation, and preparation for written and oral assessment.
