Week 2 — Founding People and Places

Week 2 focuses on the founder most closely linked to the order, the king associated with the early setting in Jerusalem, the Temple Mount headquarters, and the crucial distinction between fact and legend.

Lesson Focus

Weekly Lessons

  1. Hugh de Payns and the first companions
  2. Baldwin II and the Kingdom of Jerusalem
  3. The Temple Mount and the early headquarters
  4. Fact versus legend
Guided Reading

Reading Focus

Students should read slowly enough to connect people, place, and purpose. Schoolmasters should emphasize evidence and the danger of repeating stories without proof.
