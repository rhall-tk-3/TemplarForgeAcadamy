Week 3 — Approval and Mission

Week 3 teaches how the order gained formal support, how Bernard of Clairvaux is linked with encouragement of the order, what makes a religious order different from an ordinary army, and the early memory timeline from 1095 to 1139.

Lesson Focus

Weekly Lessons

  1. The Council of Troyes
  2. Bernard of Clairvaux and support for the order
  3. What makes a religious order different from an ordinary army
  4. Timeline review from 1095 to 1139
Guided Reading

Reading Focus

This week should reinforce that the program is educational, charitable, historical, and moral in purpose. Students should connect names and dates in sequence rather than memorize them as isolated facts.

Readiness Goals

Why This Week Matters

  • Recognize formal approval and support
  • Understand the order’s religious identity
  • Retell the early timeline in correct order
