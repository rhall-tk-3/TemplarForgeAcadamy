Parent Section

This section helps parents understand the purpose of Squire reading, how to support weekly study at home, and how to encourage steady and age-appropriate growth in virtue, service, and truthful historical learning.

How Parents Can Help

  • Review the weekly page with your student and open the linked readings together.
  • Help your student keep a calm reading routine rather than cramming before review.
  • Encourage respectful discussion, honest answers, and questions when something is unclear.
  • Support reading-log completion, memory review, and service reflection.

What Parents Should Expect

The Squire track is designed to be age-appropriate, youth-safe, and moderated. Students are being formed in history, discipline, virtue, service, and truthful understanding rather than fantasy or militarized imitation.

See the Curriculum Handbook and Required Reading List for the reading structure.

At Home

Reading Rhythm

Set aside a regular time each week for one reading selection, brief review, and one short discussion prompt.

Character

Support Conduct

Encourage honesty, respect, composure, and follow-through. These matter as much as memorized facts.

Service

Notice Growth

Ask your student how service, helpfulness, and discipline were practiced during the week.

Parent note: Final readiness is connected to attendance, memory and reading completion, written review, oral review, service hours, and conduct. Families can help most by encouraging steady habits rather than pressure or performance-only preparation.
