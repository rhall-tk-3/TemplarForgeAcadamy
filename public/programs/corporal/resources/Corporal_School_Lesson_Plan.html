Program Overview & Standards

Welcome to the Corporal School of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ (KTKC). This 8-week curriculum is designed for adult members who have successfully completed the Levie program. While the Levie program established the modern rules, Christian identity, and basic origins of the Order, the Corporal track dives deeper into the historical weight, organizational growth, and eventual suppression of the original Knights Templar.

Assessment Alignment: Throughout this program, Corporal candidates will be tested on factual historical knowledge, Christian judgment (distinguishing history from legend), and understanding the responsibilities of rank. Corporals are expected to demonstrate spiritual discipline, accountability, and a commitment to truthful witness rather than the glorification of violence.
