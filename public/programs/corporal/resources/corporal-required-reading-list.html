Corporal Required Reading List

This week-by-week reading list supports the Corporal School 8-week program. It combines internal program documents with historical articles so members can study the rise, structure, growth, and fall of the Templars while also reflecting on Christian virtues and the responsibilities of the Corporal rank.

Program emphasis: Why the Templars were founded, how they grew, why they fell, and how a Corporal should respond with truth, discipline, humility, service, accountability, and mature judgment.
