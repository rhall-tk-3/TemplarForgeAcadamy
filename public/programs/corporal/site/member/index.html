Member Guidance

This section applies the Corporal course to adult rank responsibility, Christian conduct, and readiness for greater service in the Order.

Corporal Responsibilities

  • Complete the full 8-week class requirement.
  • Remain current with all assigned classwork.
  • Attend meetings, Bible studies, and Round Tables regularly.
  • Show active fellowship and social interaction with other members.
  • Demonstrate conduct becoming of one preparing for greater responsibility and honor.

Source: Promotion Manual and By-Laws.

Christian Virtues for a Corporal

  • Truth and honorable speech
  • Prayer and meditation on Scripture
  • Discipline and sobriety
  • Courageous Christian witness
  • Charity, mercy, justice, humility, and accountability
  • Service before privilege and responsibility before status

Source: KTKC Rules and Statutes.

Corporal formation principle: In this rank, history is not merely information. It is meant to shape judgment, conduct, and readiness for responsibility. The student should grow in both historical understanding and visible Christian steadiness.
