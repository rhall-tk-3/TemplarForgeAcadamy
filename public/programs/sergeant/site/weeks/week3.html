Week 3: Organization, Offices, Provinces, and Internal Classes

Week 3 studies the Templars as an organized international order. It highlights the Grand Master, provinces, preceptories, and the internal classes of knights, sergeants, and chaplains, stressing that structure existed for mission, not privilege.

Lesson Focus Areas

  • The Grand Master and Central Command
  • Provinces and Preceptories
  • Internal Classes: Knights, Sergeants, Chaplains
  • Structure as a Tool for Service

Required Reading

  • Sergeant School — 8 Week Lesson Plan Program
  • Britannica: Templars
  • Weekly Lesson-Plan Packet
  • KTKC Rules and Statutes

Guided Focus

Focus especially on the historical role of sergeants within the order and on how hierarchy, provinces, and local houses supported discipline, logistics, and service-centered leadership.

Grand MasterPreceptorySergeantsChaplainsDiscipline

Memory Targets

  • The order was governed through a structured hierarchy and provincial network.
  • Historical sergeants were essential to manpower, logistics, and disciplined support.
  • Rank exists for responsibility and service rather than social privilege.

Readiness Goals

  • Describe the major offices and classes of the order.
  • Explain the historical importance of sergeants within Templar structure.
  • Apply humility and discipline to modern Sergeant formation.
