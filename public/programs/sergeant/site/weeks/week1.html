Week 1: From Corporal to Sergeant — Historical Review, Scope, and Source-Based Method

Week 1 transitions members from basic historical awareness into disciplined, source-based Sergeant study. It reviews the Corporal foundation while stressing truth, accountability, and rejection of myth in favor of documented history.

Lesson Focus Areas

  • Review of Corporal Foundations
  • The Importance of Documented History
  • Differentiating Speculation from Fact
  • The Sergeant’s Scope of Study

Required Reading

  • Sergeant School — 8 Week Lesson Plan Program

Guided Focus

Review the core Templar timeline learned in Corporal school, then move into documented history, careful source use, and the Sergeant duty to reject pseudo-history and sensationalism.

Memory Targets

  • Sergeant study deepens the Corporal historical foundation through source-based method.
  • Truthful history requires evidence, chronology, and disciplined judgment.
  • Friday the 13th, Grail legends, and conspiracies must be taught carefully and critically.

Readiness Goals

  • Explain why source-based study matters for Christian leadership.
  • Distinguish documented history from later invention.
  • See Sergeant formation as a call to truth, discipline, and mature judgment.
