Week 7: Philip IV, Clement V, Accusations, Torture, and Suppression

Week 7 examines the final crisis of the order through political pressure, accusations of heresy, coercive confessions, papal weakness, and the suppression of 1312. It emphasizes truth, justice, discernment, and moral seriousness.

Lesson Focus Areas

  • Philip IV and the Political Attack on the Templars
  • Friday, October 13, 1307 and the Arrests
  • Torture, Confession, and the Question of Justice
  • Clement V, Vienne, and the Suppression of 1312

Required Reading

  • Sergeant School — 8 Week Lesson Plan Program
  • Britannica: Clement V
  • HISTORY: Friday the 13th and the Knights Templar
  • National Geographic: Friday the 13th and Templar Myth

Guided Focus

Study the 1307 arrests, coercive methods, the role of Philip IV, and the weakness of Clement V under political pressure. Keep the focus on documented history rather than sensationalism.

Philip IVClement V13071312Justice

Memory Targets

  • Mass arrests in France occurred on Friday, October 13, 1307.
  • Clement V suppressed the order in 1312 under heavy pressure from Philip IV.
  • Confessions obtained under torture must be treated with moral caution and historical care.

Readiness Goals

  • Explain the political and moral dimensions of the suppression.
  • Distinguish coercion from trustworthy evidence.
  • Deepen commitment to truth, justice, and sober judgment.
