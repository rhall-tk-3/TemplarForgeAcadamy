Member Guidance

This section applies the Sergeant course to adult rank responsibility, Christian conduct, truthfulness, and readiness for greater service in the Order.

Sergeant Responsibilities

  • Complete the full 8-week class requirement.
  • Remain current with all assigned classwork.
  • Attend meetings, Bible studies, and Round Tables regularly.
  • Show active fellowship and participation in the Order.
  • Demonstrate faithfulness, discipline, and conduct becoming of one preparing for greater responsibility.

Source: Promotion Manual and By-Laws.

Christian Virtues for a Sergeant

  • Truth and honorable speech
  • Prayer and meditation on Scripture
  • Discipline and sobriety
  • Justice, mercy, humility, and accountability
  • Courageous Christian witness
  • Service before privilege and responsibility before status

Source: KTKC Rules and Statutes.

Sergeant formation principle: In this rank, history must produce judgment. The student should grow in factual understanding, moral seriousness, disciplined participation, and service-centered leadership.
