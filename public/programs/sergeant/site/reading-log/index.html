Reading Log

This page gives direct access to the week-by-week Sergeant reading log structure and explains how it should be used alongside the required reading.

Sergeant Reading Log

The reading log is designed for adult members and includes weekly checkbox tracking, note fields, discussion preparation, fellowship review, and Schoolmaster initials.

Open Required Reading

How to Use the Log

  • Complete the weekly reading before discussion.
  • Review memory targets, key figures, and source questions.
  • Record notes, questions, and reflections.
  • Track attendance and fellowship participation.
  • Ask the Schoolmaster to initial the week after review.
Week 1

From Corporal to Sergeant — Historical Review, Scope, and Source-Based Method

Review the core Templar timeline learned in Corporal school, then move into documented history, careful source use, and the Sergeant duty to reject pseudo-history and sensationalism.

Week 2

Expansion, Patronage, and Legitimacy in Europe

Study how donations, patronage, and papal approval helped the order grow, while considering the risks that accompany influence, legitimacy, and expanding resources.

Week 3

Organization, Offices, Provinces, and Internal Classes

Focus especially on the historical role of sergeants within the order and on how hierarchy, provinces, and local houses supported discipline, logistics, and service-centered leadership.

Match week page
Week 4

Military Operations, Logistics, Command, and Disciplined Service

Study the Templars within the broader Crusader setting, with attention to military logistics, command roles, disciplined conduct, and the distinction between spiritual witness and romanticized militarism.

Match week page
Week 5

Finance, Stewardship, Administration, and Public Trust

Learn how the Templars became trusted administrators and financiers, and connect that history to modern duties of integrity, transparency, stewardship, and resistance to mission drift.

Match week page
Week 6

Strategic Strain, Acre, and the Crisis of Purpose

Study Acre, the larger Crusading decline, and the weakening of the order’s original purpose. Consider how loss of mission clarity creates vulnerability both historically and spiritually.

Match week page
Week 7

Philip IV, Clement V, Accusations, Torture, and Suppression

Study the 1307 arrests, coercive methods, the role of Philip IV, and the weakness of Clement V under political pressure. Keep the focus on documented history rather than sensationalism.

Match week page
Week 8

Jacques de Molay, Hospitallers, Legacy, Myth versus History, and Sergeant Readiness

Review what the Templars actually were, how Jacques de Molay fits into the final narrative, why the Hospitallers help illuminate continuity and contrast, and why Sergeant-level study must reject fantasy in favor of truth.
