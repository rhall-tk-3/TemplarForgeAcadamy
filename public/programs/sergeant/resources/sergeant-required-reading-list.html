Sergeant Required Reading List

This week-by-week reading list supports the Sergeant School 8-week program. It combines internal program documents with historical articles so members can study the Templars through source-based history while reflecting on Christian virtues, Sergeant duties, and leadership accountability.

Program emphasis: disciplined historical method, organizational maturity, military and administrative function, financial stewardship, suppression, myth versus history, and adult Sergeant readiness.
