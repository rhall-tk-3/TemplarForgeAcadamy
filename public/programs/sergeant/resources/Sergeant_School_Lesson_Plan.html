Sergeant School — 8 Week Lesson Plan Program

1. Program Overview

This Sergeant School program follows the foundational Corporal program, transitioning members from basic historical awareness to a mature, source-based study of the Knights Templar. The curriculum deepens understanding of the Order's organizational maturity, military logistics, administrative structure, financial stewardship, and eventual suppression. By grounding study in documented history rather than myth, the program prepares candidates for adult moral leadership and greater responsibility within the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ (KTKC).

2. Program Standards

In accordance with the KTKC Promotion Manual and By-Laws, candidates for the rank of Sergeant must meet the following expectations:

  • Complete the 8-week class requirement in its entirety.
  • Remain current with all assigned classwork and readings.
  • Maintain regular attendance at meetings, Bible studies, and Round Tables.
  • Demonstrate active fellowship and participation in the daily life of the Order.
  • Achieve advancement based on demonstrated faithfulness, discipline, participation, and completion of requirements.
  • Acknowledge that deficiencies in conduct or coursework may delay promotion, subject to Board review.

3. Christian Virtues and Formation

A Sergeant is held to a higher standard of visible virtue. The curriculum emphasizes the cultivation of true Christian knighthood, specifically focusing on:

Core Virtues: Truth, charity, discipline, courage, justice, mercy, humility, and accountability.
Spiritual Disciplines: Fervent prayer, devotion to Scripture, personal integrity, sobriety, and service.
Community Witness: Courageous Christian witness and the fostering of brotherhood and sisterhood in charity.

Week 5: Finance, Stewardship, Administration, and Public Trust

Weekly Overview: This week analyzes the Templars' role as medieval bankers and administrators, exploring how their reputation for honesty created immense wealth, and how KTKC views financial integrity.

Lessons:
Lesson 1: The Origins of Templar Banking
Lesson 2: Treasure Transport and Letters of Credit
Lesson 3: The Dangers of Wealth and Influence
Lesson 4: KTKC Financial Integrity and Stewardship

Discussion Questions:
1. How did the Templars' initial reputation for poverty and discipline ironically lead to immense wealth?
2. Why do KTKC Statutes strictly prohibit the buying or selling of ranks?

Virtues/Emphases: Stewardship, integrity, justice.
Sergeant Responsibility Connection: Sergeants must model absolute integrity, understanding that public trust is easily lost and that leadership is never a commodity.
