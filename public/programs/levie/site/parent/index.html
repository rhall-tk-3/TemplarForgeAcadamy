Parent Section

This section helps parents support Levie reading, discussion, and steady completion of the weekly reading log during the 8-week probationary class period.

How Parents Can Help

  • Review the weekly reading focus and memory targets with the student.
  • Encourage calm, truthful discussion instead of rushed memorization.
  • Help the student complete the weekly portion of the reading log.
  • Support attendance and steady completion of the probationary course.

What Parents Should Expect

The Levie program is formal, Christian, and non-militaristic. The first half of the course focuses on rules, conduct, and order identity; the second half introduces only the early historical background that leads to the beginning of the Templars.
