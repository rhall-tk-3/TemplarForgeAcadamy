Week 2 — Standards, Duties, and Equality

Week 2 develops the Levie’s understanding of Christian fellowship, the rejection of corrupt practices, liberty of conscience, open promises, and the standards that govern equality, duties, and attendance.

Lesson Structure

Lesson Focus Areas

  1. Ecumenical Fellowship
  2. Rejection of False Worship and Corrupt Practice
  3. Liberty of Conscience and Moral Obedience
  4. Oaths, Vows, and Solemn Promises
Guided Reading

Reading Focus

Understand the duties of members, financial integrity, equality among members regardless of social status, and the mandatory attendance expectations for promotion.

Memory Targets

What to Remember

  • Ranks cannot be bought or sold.
  • Promotion requires completion of classwork and regular attendance.
  • Members are accountable to Christian truth, Scripture, and lawful conduct.
Readiness Goals

Why This Week Matters

  • Accept the standards of participation, equality, and financial integrity.
  • Understand that promises must be open and morally sound.
  • Recognize the seriousness of attendance and coursework completion.
