Reading Log

Levie Student Reading Log

The reading log includes student information fields, weekly checkbox tracking, instructor initials, notes and reflection space, and a final completion summary.

Open Student Reading Log Open Updated Reading List

How to Use the Log

  • Complete the weekly reading before class discussion.
  • Review the memory targets listed for the week.
  • Record notes, reflections, or questions in the provided space.
  • Ask the Schoolmaster to initial the week after review.
  • Complete the final summary at the end of Week 8.
