Program Purpose

The Levie School provides the foundational entry-level formation for new members of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ. It covers Christian identity, essential rules of conduct, membership duties, and the early historical origins that lead up to the beginning of the Templars.

Sources: Levie School — 8 Week Lesson Plan Program, Updated Levie Required Reading List, Rules and Statutes, Promotion Manual and By-Laws.

How This Section Is Organized

  • Weeks: one page for each week with lesson focus areas, reading emphasis, key terms, memory targets, and readiness goals.
  • Required Reading: the updated reading list placed in a week-by-week website view.
  • Reading Log: direct access to the student-facing editable weekly log.
  • Parent Section: practical help for reading support, discussion, and steady habits.
  • Schoolmaster Section: staff-facing links to the statutes, by-laws, lesson plan, and review documents.

Week-by-Week Path

Each weekly page aligns with the updated reading list and emphasizes entry-level readiness, orderly conduct, and only the early historical background needed for the Levie rank.

Week 1

Identity, Purpose, and Consecration

Week 1 establishes the Christian identity of the Order, its consecration to Jesus Christ, its mission of truthful Christian service, and the probationary standards that shape the Levie class period.

Open Week 1
Week 2

Standards, Duties, and Equality

Week 2 develops the Levie’s understanding of Christian fellowship, the rejection of corrupt practices, liberty of conscience, open promises, and the standards that govern equality, duties, and attendance.

Open Week 2
Week 3

Conduct, Leadership, and Accountability

Week 3 teaches the Levie to view rank as service, not privilege, and to understand integrity, equality, duties, leadership standards, and complaint or redress under Christian accountability.

Open Week 3
Week 4

The Five Governing Pillars

Week 4 gathers the moral and spiritual core of the Levie program by emphasizing service, prayer, discipline, witness, charity, spiritual warfare as spiritual witness, and the life of sacrificial fellowship.

Open Week 4
Week 5

The Need for Protection (Early Pre-Templar History)

Week 5 begins the historical portion of the Levie program by introducing the importance of the Holy Land to medieval Christians, the context of the First Crusade, and the dangers faced by pilgrims on the roads to Jerusalem.

Open Week 5
Week 6

Founding People and Places

Week 6 introduces the figures and places most closely connected to the beginning of the Order, especially Hugh de Payns, King Baldwin II, and the Temple Mount in Jerusalem.

Open Week 6
Week 7

Official Approval and Early Support

Week 7 shows how the early Order moved from informal beginning to formal recognition, and how support from important church figures helped establish legitimacy and mission.

Open Week 7
Week 8

Religious-Order Identity and Early Timeline Review

Week 8 completes the Levie historical introduction by reviewing the early timeline and clarifying what distinguished the early Templars from an ordinary army: prayer, discipline, service, and religious identity.

Open Week 8
