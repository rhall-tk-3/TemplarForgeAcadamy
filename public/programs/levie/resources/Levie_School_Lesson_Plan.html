Levie School

8 Week Lesson Plan Program

Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ

Program Overview

The Levie School provides the foundational entry-level formation for new members of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ (KTKC). This 8-week curriculum is designed to instruct the Levie in the Order’s Christian identity, essential rules of conduct, membership duties, and early historical origins. The tone of this instruction is formal, Christian, and non-militaristic, focusing on spiritual discipline, integrity, and truthful historical understanding.

Program Standards and Format

In accordance with the Promotion Manual and By-Laws, members entering the rank of Levie shall serve a probationary class period of sixty (60) days, recognized as an eight (8) week program. During this period, Levies are required to complete instruction in the Order's Rules, Regulations, and Operating Procedures. Promotion is not automatic; it requires the completion of required class instruction within the prescribed timeframe. Levies must maintain regular attendance at scheduled meetings, Bible studies, and General Order Meetings (Round Tables). All attendance is logged and recorded. A deficiency shall be noted if a member fails to complete assigned coursework or maintain regular attendance. Upon completion, the Board of Promotion Review evaluates the Levie based on coursework completion, attendance logs, participation, conduct becoming of a member, and readiness for advancement.

Assessment Alignment

This program is strictly aligned with the five assessment pillars: Knowledge (grasping foundational rules and early timeline), Judgment (separating truth from legend and understanding moral obedience), Service (commitment to charity and order duties), Character (demonstrating discipline, sobriety, and honesty), and Communication (clarity in written assessments and oral reviews). Progress is measured through written assessment (weekly multiple-choice questions), reading and memory completion, conduct review, attendance tracking, and final board readiness.
