Levie Required Reading List

Welcome to the entry-level formation for the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ (KTKC). This document outlines the required reading and study expectations for the rank of Levie. The following reading list is designed to support your 60-day (8-week) probationary class period, ensuring you understand the fundamental rules, regulations, operating procedures, and early history of the Order.

How Levie Reading List Works

As a Levie serving your initial probationary period, your reading assignments are structured to support the official 8-week class instruction. The first four weeks are dedicated entirely to the Order’s Rules, Statutes, By-Laws, conduct, and operating procedures. The final four weeks are dedicated exclusively to the early history leading up to the formation of the historical Knights Templar. You are expected to read the assigned texts carefully prior to each week's instruction and be prepared to participate in class discussions and written quizzes.

Reading Log Requirement

Consistent participation and coursework completion are primary metrics for determining readiness for advancement. Every Levie must maintain an official Reading Log. This log serves as proof of your coursework completion and active participation. Failure to complete the required readings and log them during the 60-day probationary period constitutes a deficiency, which will be noted by the Board of Promotion Review and may delay your advancement.

Schoolmaster Guidance

This curriculum must be overseen with care. Schoolmasters should verify the Levie Reading Log weekly and ensure attendance is accurately recorded. Parents and instructors must facilitate age-appropriate, moderated discussions regarding the rules, Christian virtues, and historical facts. The goal is to build a foundation of honesty, spiritual identity, and historical truth without glorifying violence or encouraging the spread of unverified legends.

Week 1: Identity, Purpose, and Consecration

  • Required Reading: KTKC Rules and Statutes, Articles I, II, III, and IV.
  • Guided Reading Focus: Focus on the Christian nature of the Order, its consecration to Jesus Christ, and the foundational mission of honoring God through true virtues of Christian knighthood.
  • Key Terms or Figures: KTKC (Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ), The Holy Trinity, Ecumenical Fellowship.
  • Memory Targets: The Order is forever consecrated to Jesus Christ; its purpose is to defend the faith through truthful, nonviolent witness.
  • Readiness Goals: Demonstrate an understanding of the Order's Christian identity and its rejection of false worship.

Week 2: Standards, Duties, and Equality

  • Required Reading: KTKC Rules and Statutes, Articles IX, X, XI, and XII; Promotion Manual By-Laws, Articles I, II, and III.
  • Guided Reading Focus: Understand the duties of members, financial integrity, equality among members regardless of social status, and the mandatory attendance expectations for promotion.
  • Key Terms or Figures: Equality and Fairness, Round Tables (General Order Meetings), Probationary Period.
  • Memory Targets: Ranks cannot be bought or sold; promotion requires successful completion of the 8-week class instruction and regular attendance at Bible studies and meetings.
  • Readiness Goals: Accept the personal responsibility of attending required meetings and submitting to the Order's standard of financial and moral integrity.

Week 3: Conduct, Leadership, and Accountability

  • Required Reading: KTKC Rules and Statutes, Articles VI, VII, VIII, XIII, and XIV.
  • Guided Reading Focus: Examine the standards of leadership, liberty of conscience, the nature of open vows versus secret oaths, and the procedures for complaint and redress without fear of retaliation.
  • Key Terms or Figures: Liberty of Conscience, Discipline and Redress, Standard of Leadership.
  • Memory Targets: No superior can command an act violating Scripture or civil law; leaders are held to a higher standard of visible virtue and humility.
  • Readiness Goals: Understand how accountability functions within the Order and recognize that no rank shields a member from correction.

Week 4: The Five Governing Pillars

  • Required Reading: KTKC Rules and Statutes, Articles XV, XVI, and XVII.
  • Guided Reading Focus: Reflect on the five pillars of the Order: Service, Prayer, Discipline, Witness, and Charity. Understand the concept of spiritual warfare and the practice of sacrificial brotherhood and sisterhood.
  • Key Terms or Figures: The Five Governing Pillars, Spiritual Warfare, Sacrificial Love.
  • Memory Targets: Memorize the Five Pillars: Service to Christ, Prayer and Holy Meditation, Discipline and Virtue, Courageous Christian Witness, and Brotherhood/Sisterhood in Charity.
  • Readiness Goals: Internalize the core values required to transition from a probationary Levie to an active contributing member of the fellowship.

Week 5: The Need for Protection (Early Pre-Templar History)

  • Required Reading: Levie History Handout 1 (Christian Pilgrimage and The First Crusade Context).
  • Guided Reading Focus: Learn why the Holy Land mattered to medieval Christians and understand the real dangers faced by travelers and pilgrims on the roads to Jerusalem.
  • Key Terms or Figures: Christian Pilgrims, The First Crusade (1095–1099), Jerusalem.
  • Memory Targets: The First Crusade established fragile Crusader states; travelers to Jerusalem faced constant threats, creating a dire need for organized protection.
  • Readiness Goals: Accurately explain the historical conditions and context that existed immediately before the Templar Order was formed.

Week 6: Founding People and Places

  • Required Reading: Levie History Handout 2 (Hugh de Payns and the Early Headquarters); Simple Biographies.
  • Guided Reading Focus: Discover the actual founders of the Order, their original protective purpose, and the significance of their first headquarters.
  • Key Terms or Figures: Hugh de Payns, King Baldwin II, The Temple Mount.
  • Memory Targets: Hugh de Payns is the founder most closely linked with the Order (circa 1119/1120); King Baldwin II provided their early headquarters on the Temple Mount.
  • Readiness Goals: Identify the key founding figures and correctly state the founding purpose: to protect pilgrims, rather than to seek wealth or glory.

Week 7: Official Approval and Early Support

  • Required Reading: Levie History Handout 3 (The Council of Troyes and Bernard of Clairvaux).
  • Guided Reading Focus: Study how the early Order gained official church recognition, legitimacy, and the spiritual backing of influential contemporary leaders.
  • Key Terms or Figures: The Council of Troyes (1128/1129), Bernard of Clairvaux.
  • Memory Targets: The Council of Troyes provided formal approval for the Order; Bernard of Clairvaux was a key spiritual supporter, not a founder.
  • Readiness Goals: Differentiate between the informal founding of the Order and its formal, official recognition by the wider church.

Week 8: Religious-Order Identity and Early Timeline Review

  • Required Reading: Levie History Handout 4 (Religious Order Identity and Early Timeline Review).
  • Guided Reading Focus: Understand what separated the early Templars from an ordinary medieval army—specifically their daily discipline, prayer, and service. Review the early timeline facts.
  • Key Terms or Figures: Religious Order Identity, Service Before Self.
  • Memory Targets: Recall the timeline from 1095 (First Crusade context) to the Council of Troyes (1128/1129); understand that the Order was a religious body requiring prayer and discipline.
  • Readiness Goals: Complete the historical timeline review and demonstrate preparedness for the final probationary written quiz and oral evaluation.

Note on Promotion Readiness: Completion of this 8-week reading curriculum is a strict requirement for advancement, but it does not guarantee automatic promotion. The Board of Promotion Review will evaluate each Levie based upon coursework completion, logged attendance, active participation, and a demonstrated conduct becoming of a member of the Knights Templar of the Kingdom of Christ. Continue to study diligently and apply these principles to your daily walk.
