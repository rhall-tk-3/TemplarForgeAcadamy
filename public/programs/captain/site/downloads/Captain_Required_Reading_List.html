Captain Required Reading List

Officer Formation Level II

This document outlines the 12-week reading path for Captain School. The curriculum is specifically focused on the realities of local command, stewardship, delegated authority, mentoring lieutenants, pastoral care, administration, discipline, and practicum readiness. As an Officer Formation Level II course, it prepares leaders to transition from standard task execution to the faithful stewardship of a body.

Format Note: This HTML document format was chosen because it best supports the branded, printable layout required for KTKC school program reading lists, ensuring a clean, WYSIWYG experience when printing or saving as a PDF.
