Member Guidance

This section connects Sergeant First Class study to conduct, service, brotherhood, and preparation for Knight Aspirant.

SFC Responsibilities

  • Complete the full 8-week course.
  • Remain current with reading and classwork.
  • Attend regularly and participate faithfully.
  • Demonstrate visible discipline, humility, and charity.
  • Prepare seriously for Knight Aspirant and the required essay.

Sources: Promotion Manual and By-Laws, KTKC Rules and Statutes.

Leadership Direction

  • Govern without partiality.
  • Correct without cruelty.
  • Listen without arrogance.
  • Serve without seeking privilege.
  • Carry truth, charity, and witness into public life.
SFC formation principle: this rank is not only historical study. It is leadership formation designed to test whether knowledge is producing discipline, service, discernment, and readiness for deeper vocation.
