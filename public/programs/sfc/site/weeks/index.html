Weekly Lesson Pages

Browse the week-by-week progression of the Sergeant First Class program.

Week 1

From Sergeant to Sergeant First Class

Week 1 reviews the true historical Templars and introduces the Sergeant First Class rank as a step into more visible leadership, mature judgment, and preparation for Knight Aspirant.

Open Week 1
Week 2

Suppression, Legacy, and Historical Boundary

Week 2 revisits the fall of the medieval order and clarifies where documented history ends and later legend begins.

Open Week 2
Week 3

Modern Templar Revivals and Continuity Claims

Week 3 studies the post-medieval revival of Templar identity, especially the modern order tradition associated with 1804, while maintaining honesty about continuity claims.

Open Week 3
Week 4

True Templar Virtues and Masonic Templar Orders

Week 4 compares Christian virtue-centered Templar formation with the Masonic York Rite Knights Templar, while maintaining historical clarity and charitable precision.

Open Week 4
Week 5

Sergeant First Class Duty, Rank, and Leadership Development

Week 5 focuses on the actual responsibilities of a Sergeant First Class: faithfulness, attendance, fellowship, disciplined conduct, and readiness to shepherd others by example.

Open Week 5
Week 6

Battles, Courage, and the Power of Faith

Week 6 uses key battle case studies to show how morale, courage, endurance, and faith shaped decision-making and witness in times of extreme pressure.

Open Week 6
Week 7

Modern Christian Witness, Charity, and Public Service

Week 7 connects the Templar ideal to modern service, brotherhood, truthful speech, charity, and disciplined public witness.

Open Week 7
Week 8

Knight Aspirant Readiness and Essay Preparation

Week 8 prepares the member for the next course by emphasizing monk-like discipline, internal formation, and the required essay on what a Templar Knight means.
