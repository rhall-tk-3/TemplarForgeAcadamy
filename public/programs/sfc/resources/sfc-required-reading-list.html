Sergeant First Class Required Reading List

This concise week-by-week list continues from the Sergeant program into post-medieval Templar legacy, modern Templar organizations, Christian virtue and leadership, battle case studies, and preparation for Knight Aspirant.

Core use each week: Sergeant First Class School 8-Week Lesson Plan Program
Knight Aspirant essay ahead: “What Does a Templar Knight Mean to You?”
Week Required Reading Focus
Week 1
From Sergeant to SFC		 SFC Lesson Plan
Britannica: Templars
KTKC Rules and Statutes 		Review the true historical Templars, the transition into SFC rank, and the Christian virtue standard for visible leadership.
Week 2
Suppression, legacy, and historical boundary		 Britannica: Clement V
HISTORY: Friday the 13th
National Geographic: Templar myth 		Study the end of the medieval order and learn where documented history stops and later legend begins.
Week 3
Modern Templar revivals		 OSMTH: Modern Order History
Britannica: Templars
Curriculum Handbook 		Trace how modern groups adopted Templar language, especially the 1804 revival tradition, without claiming direct medieval continuity.
Week 4
True Templar virtues vs. Masonic Templar orders		 Britannica: Freemasonry Facts
York Rite: Knights Templar
Grand Encampment: About 		Understand the difference between Christian virtue-centered Templar formation and the Masonic York Rite structure, including Britannica’s note that Freemasonry has no historical connection to the medieval Templars.
Week 5
SFC rank, duty, and leadership		 KTKC Rules and Statutes
Promotion Manual and By-Laws
SFC Lesson Plan 		Focus on SFC responsibilities: discipline, attendance, fellowship, correction without cruelty, and leadership by service rather than privilege.
Week 6
Battles, courage, and the power of faith		 ThoughtCo: Battle of Montgisard
Britannica: Battle of Hattin
Britannica: Crusades 		Use Montgisard and Hattin as case studies in morale, discipline, leadership, endurance, and the role faith played in courage under pressure.
Week 7
Modern Christian witness and public service		 KTKC Rules and Statutes
OSMTH: Modern Order History
Promotion Manual and By-Laws 		Connect Templar identity to charity, discipline, brotherhood, truthful witness, and leadership in modern society.
Week 8
Knight Aspirant readiness and essay preparation		 Promotion Manual and By-Laws
KTKC Rules and Statutes
SFC Lesson Plan 		Prepare for Knight Aspirant by reviewing monk-like discipline, modern virtue application, and the essay prompt on what a Templar Knight means to you.

Historical references used for this reading path include Britannica on the Templars, Clement V, the Crusades, and Hattin; HISTORY and National Geographic on the suppression narrative and myth; official York Rite and Grand Encampment pages for modern Masonic Templarism; and OSMTH for the 1804 modern-order revival.
