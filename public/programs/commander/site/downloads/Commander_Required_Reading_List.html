Commander Required Reading List

Officer Formation Level IV

This document outlines a concise 12-week reading plan aligned to senior command, doctrine, judgment, institutional stewardship, officer alignment, crisis leadership, succession, and practicum readiness. It forms the intellectual and practical foundation for transitioning from regional steward to institutional guardian.

Core Course Documents

  • Commander School Syllabus PDF: /documents/commander/commander-lesson-plan.docx

Weekly Reading Plan

Week 1: The Burden of Senior Command

Focus: Understanding the weight of stewardship, sacrifice, and readiness at the highest command level.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Introduction & Objectives
  • Fordham Primitive Rule of the Templars - Sections on Obedience and Devotion

Week 2: Senior Command in the Knights Templar

Focus: Examining the historical structure and hierarchy of senior Templar command.
  • Wikipedia: Knights Templar - Organization and Leadership
  • Britannica: Templars - History and Structure

Week 3: The Offices of Marshal and Seneschal

Focus: The distinct roles of the highest military and administrative deputies.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Historical Models (Marshal & Seneschal)
  • OSMTH Modern Order History - Administrative Evolution

Week 4: Doctrine, Mission, and Institutional Identity

Focus: Preserving the Order's founding purpose and governing doctrine.
  • Fordham Primitive Rule of the Templars - Establishing Order Identity
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 4 Framework

Week 5: Authority, Judgment, and Command Presence

Focus: Exercising broad delegated authority with disciplined, measured decision-making.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 5 Framework
  • Wikipedia: Knights Templar - Code of Conduct and Authority

Week 6: Senior Officer Alignment and Direction

Focus: Creating unity under authority and directing regional officers effectively.
  • OSMTH Modern Order History - Legacy of Institutional Unity
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 6 Framework

Week 7: Crisis Leadership and Difficult Decisions

Focus: Maintaining stability, morale, and doctrinal fidelity under intense pressure.
  • Britannica: Templars - Decline and Historical Crises
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 7 Framework

Week 8: Succession, Appointments, and Officer Formation

Focus: Pipeline stewardship and preparing the next generation of commanders.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 8 Framework

Week 9: Discipline, Correction, and Justice at the Highest Officer Level

Focus: Ensuring moral governance, strict accountability, and restorative justice.
  • Fordham Primitive Rule of the Templars - Sections on Faults and Penance
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 9 Framework

Week 10: Stewardship of Culture, Communication, and Morale

Focus: Sustaining institutional health and transparent Order-wide communication.
  • OSMTH Modern Order History - Cultural Stewardship
  • Commander School Syllabus - Lesson 10 Framework

Week 11: Senior Command Practicum

Focus: Preparation for strategic execution, command assessment, and practical leadership.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Practicum Guidelines & Expectations

Week 12: Capstone Preparation and Commander’s Board Review

Focus: Integration of all formation lessons for the oral board and final review.
  • Commander School Syllabus - Evaluation Criteria and Board Review
  • Britannica: Templars - Final Review of Templar Legacy

Practical Written Work and Leadership Use

  • Doctrine Papers: Readings on the Primitive Rule ensure written statements align with foundational Templar values.
  • Command Plans: Historical hierarchy models provide frameworks for structuring modern command alignment.
  • Crisis Exercises: Reviews of historical pressures prepare the candidate for case-study responses and operational resilience.
  • Succession Mapping: Stewardship principles guide the identification and training of capable junior officers.
  • Practicum Execution: Course materials ground the candidate’s real-world command initiatives in established procedure.
  • Oral Board Readiness: Synthesizing these texts prepares the Commander to articulate clear, doctrinally sound judgments under review.
