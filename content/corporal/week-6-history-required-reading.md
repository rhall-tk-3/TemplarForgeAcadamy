# Week 6 - History Required Reading

## Required Reading

Read: [Templars - Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Templars)

## Primary Focus

The fall of Acre in 1291, the loss of the Holy Land, and the Templars' search for a new purpose.

## Key Historical Subjects

The collapse of the Crusader states, the Templars' relocation to Cyprus, the question of a new crusade, and the failure to find a viable new mission.

## Formation Emphasis

How an institution whose identity is tied to a mission must either adapt or fracture when that mission disappears.

## Readiness Goal

Explain what the fall of Acre meant for the Templars and why their inability to redefine their mission left them exposed.

## Historical Snapshot

Britannica records that in 1291, the fall of Acre — the last major Christian stronghold in the Holy Land — stripped the military orders of their primary theater of operations. The Templars withdrew to Cyprus. The loss of the Holy Land created a fundamental challenge: the Order's entire identity was built around protecting pilgrims and defending Crusader territory. With those territories gone, European rulers and Church officials began to question the justification for the Templars' vast wealth, privileges, and independence. Proposals for merging the military orders circulated. The Order had no compelling answer to the question of what it now existed to do.

## Key Themes

- 1291 was not merely a military defeat. It was an identity crisis.
- Survival after the loss of a primary mission requires a coherent new purpose.
- Institutions that cannot answer "why do we exist now?" lose their claim to resources and protection.

## Formation Insight

The Templars' fall did not begin in 1307. It began in 1291, when the conditions that justified their existence collapsed and no new justification replaced them. An order that cannot adapt its mission becomes a monument to a past that no longer needs it.

## Readiness Check

Before moving on, a corporal should be able to answer this in one clear sentence:

**Why did the fall of Acre in 1291 mark the beginning of the end for the Templar Order?**
